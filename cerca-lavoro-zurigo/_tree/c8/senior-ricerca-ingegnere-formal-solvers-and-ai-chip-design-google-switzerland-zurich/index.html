About the job The Operations Research group develops combinatorial optimization and solver technology across Google and Alphabet. Within the OR group, our Gemini + Solvers team works at the intersection of LLMs/RL, formal and optimization solvers, and TPU chip design, with a focus on RTL transformation and equivalence checking. Automated RTL optimization (for timing, area, power, or pipelining) is often bottlenecked by verification: rewrites are only useful when functional equivalence can be proven, yet arithmetic datapaths, sequential retiming, and structural transformations routinely stress standard equivalence flows. Our work spans improving Gemini's ability to propose and verify RTL transformations, scaling the underlying CP-SAT and equivalence engines (including decomposition, invariants, and ML-guided search), and tightening the interaction loop between models and solvers. Google Research addresses challenges that define the technology of today and tomorrow. From conducting fundamental research to influencing product development, our research teams have the opportunity to impact technology used by billions of people every day.

Our teams aspire to make discoveries that impact everyone, and core to our approach is sharing our research and tools to fuel progress in the field -- we publish regularly in academic journals, release projects as open source, and apply research to Google products.

Responsibilities

  • Build verification and RL harnesses where equivalence checkers and SAT/SMT/CP-SAT solvers provide exact checks, counter examples, and reward signals for Gemini on RTL generation and circuit optimization rewrites.
  • Scale combinational and sequential RTL equivalence checking via solver improvements, miter simplification, cut/subproblem decomposition, invariant generation, and ML/LLM-guided heuristics.
  • Synthesize and curate RTL datasets, transformation pairs, and evaluation suites covering realistic hardware corner cases (datapath arithmetic, pipelining, control logic).
  • Develop tool interfaces and agentic harnesses connecting Gemini with formal solvers, simulators, and hardware toolchains (Verilog/SystemVerilog, XLS, etc.).
  • Collaborate with Google hardware and AI teams to apply Gemini + solver methods to real design and verification bottlenecks.

Requisiti

  • 5 years of experience programming in Python or C++.

  • Experience in at least two of the following: formal or optimization solvers (e.g., SAT, SMT, CP-SAT, MIP), RTL design or formal hardware verification, or machine learning.
