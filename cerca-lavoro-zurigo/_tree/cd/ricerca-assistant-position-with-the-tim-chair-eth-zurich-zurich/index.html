Research Assistant Position with the TIM Chair

50%, Zurich, fixed-term

Employment: 50% for 6 months, with the possibility for extending hours and duration

Start: By agreement, ideally as soon as possible

You will be a core member of the research team, working closely with the principal investigators and postdoctoral researchers on data collection and early analysis. The position starts as soon as possible and runs through the first phase of fieldwork, with the possibility of extending hours and duration as the study develops.

Project background

This project is a field study that examines how organizations plan for ultra-long time horizons. Our empirical setting is an organization responsible for the long-term containment of radioactive waste in a deep geological repository. Planning decisions there cannot be tested and feedback is limited/absent, so the project examines how people from different occupations and temporal frames coordinate, how organizations build and sustain confidence in decisions they cannot verify, and how far-future aspirations become concrete commitments in the present. The study is a longitudinal, multi-level qualitative case study that combines ethnographic observation, interviews, and analysis of organizational documents.

Job description

Responsibilities include (but not limited to)

  • Archival analysis: collecting, organizing, and indexing organizational artefacts such as organizational charts, meeting minutes, project rosters, email threads, project management records, and safety case documentation, and building a chronological event database
  • Interviews: assisting with semi-structured interviews across occupational groups and career stages, including audio recording and transcription
  • Observation: attending design reviews, interface meetings, modelling workshops, governance meetings, and external review events, often at short notice, and writing detailed field notes on the same day
  • Data management: translating documents from German to English, maintaining informant IDs and confidentiality protocols, keeping a fieldwork log, and organizing material for later indexing and analysis
  • Analysis support: open coding (using Atlas.ti), writing analytic memos, and contributing to ideation sessions and discussions of findings

Desirable

  • Familiarity with organization theory, technology management, or related disciplines
  • Familiarity with engineering, geology, or other scientific fields
