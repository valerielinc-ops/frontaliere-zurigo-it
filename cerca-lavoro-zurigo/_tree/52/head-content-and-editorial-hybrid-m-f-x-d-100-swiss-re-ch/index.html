Descrizione completa

Are you a strategic editorial leader who transforms complex ideas into compelling stories that drive real business impact? If you thrive at the intersection of content strategy, editorial excellence and operational delivery — this is your opportunity to shape how one of the world's leading reinsurers communicates with the world. About the Role As Head Content and Editorial, you will lead Swiss Re's integrated content strategy and global editorial service across Group Communications, Branding & Marketing, Swiss Re Institute (SRI) and Public Affairs. You will own the editorial portfolio, governance and a lean pooled production team — ensuring content is strategically designed, purposefully reused and activated with precision across global audiences. Key Responsibilities

  • Content strategy: Translate business priorities across Group Communications, SRI and Public Affairs into shared editorial themes, defined outcomes and a manageable content portfolio
  • Editorial planning and delivery: Own the global editorial calendar, oversee commissioning, scheduling and quality; adapt priorities swiftly as business needs evolve
  • Content production: Lead and coach writers and editors delivering thought leadership, SRI publications, podcasts, executive speeches and campaign content
  • Editorial standards and quality: Uphold writing, editing and accessible storytelling standards; ensure rigorous fact-checking and consistent messaging across all content
  • Flagship content promotional strategy: Lead global thought-leadership campaign strategy, concepts and narratives; partner with Channels on actionable promotional plans, content assets, formats and timing for priority audiences worldwide
  • Content reuse and commercialization: Adapt core research and communications into modular assets, toolkits and localization packs for diverse stakeholders and channels
  • Cross-pillar collaboration: Partner with Business Units, Channels, Events and Platforms teams on audience targeting, formats, timing and performance-driven editorial refinement
  • People and budget management: Develop editorial talent, manage budgets and suppliers, and contribute to integrated three-pillar leadership and governance

About the Team You will be joining Swiss Re's Group Communications, Branding & Marketing function — a globally connected team shaping Swiss Re's voice across key audiences. The Content and Editorial pillar works in close partnership with Channels and Platforms, AI & Analytics to deliver integrated, insight-driven communications. The team operates as a lean, high-impact content service supporting Group Communications, SRI, Public Affairs and Business Units with world-class editorial production and thought leadership.

About You

You are a confident, collaborative content leader who combines strategic vision with hands-on editorial craft. You build trust with senior stakeholders, inspire talented writers and editors, and make smart prioritization calls in a fast-moving environment. You balance creative ambition with operational discipline, energized by making rigorous, research-based content accessible and impactful for global audiences.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Senior experience in content strategy and editorial service delivery in a complex international communications, research or professional-services environment
  • Proven ability to align senior stakeholders around outcome-led content themes, commissioning priorities and delivery models across a global matrix organization
  • Strong editorial judgment and storytelling ability , including rigorous research publishing, executive communications and high-quality specialist content
  • Demonstrated people leadership of editors and writers, with experience in prioritization and capacity planning within a lean production team
  • Solid knowledge of content governance , lifecycle management, metadata, content measurement and AI-assisted editorial operations

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience adapting and repurposing content across stakeholders, use cases and channels
  • Operational and financial management skills with a strong service mindset
  • Background in reinsurance, insurance or knowledge-intensive B2B environments
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted editorial workflows and content technology platforms
  • University degree or equivalent; excellent written and spoken English
