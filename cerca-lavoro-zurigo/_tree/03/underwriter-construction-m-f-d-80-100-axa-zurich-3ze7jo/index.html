Descrizione completa

Underwriter Construction (m/f/d) Permanent (80 - 100%) in Zurich, Switzerland From breaking ground to laying foundations to opening day, large construction projects face a wide variety of risks at every stage. As a Construction Underwriter, you are at the forefront of the significant infrastructure investment trend we observe across the globe. We’re a leading insurance provider in this segment and bring our experience and insight, deep technical expertise and risk transfer solutions to serve our clients and partners. Great underwriting starts with great people. You will find that our teams are collaborative, down-to-earth and open to the new. This is a regulated role under the Central Bank of Ireland’s Fitness and Probity regime. What you’ll be doing What will your essential responsibilities include?

  • Developing and structuring tailor-made solutions by considering the Construction Underwriting strategy and Underwriting policy to win new business and retain existing customers in the area of building construction works, green and renewable projects, oil/gas/chemical installations, power generation through to open-cut mining
  • Proactively foster positive relationship with clients to get mutual understanding of their needs
  • Advise, assist and service clients on insurance and risk management matters to ensure clients satisfaction and respond to client and AXA XL queries in a timely manner
  • Assessing the risk technically, evaluating appropriate risk premium by considering the risk costs inherent to the Construction Sector, reinsurance cessions as well as the underwriting and general administration costs to ensure profitability
  • Initiate opportunities for cross-selling
  • Ensure proper hand-over for timely implementation of new and renewed accounts according to AXA XL's quality standards (including policy-, invoice- and endorsement issuance)
  • The Underwriter Construction may have a responsibility for the development of skills, knowledge and competencies of less experienced colleagues
  • They will develop best practice and the sharing of underwriting knowledge within the team

You will report to the Underwriting Manager Construction. What you’ll bring

We’re looking for someone who has these abilities and skills

Required Skills and Abilities

  • A completed degree in a technical or economic field, or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in underwriting in the construction and/or engineering sector with strong analytical and problem solving skills
  • Fundamental knowledge of global construction underwriting; experience with large accounts is advantageous
  • Ability to develop and maintain relationships with international clients and businesspartners and excellent marketing, presentation and negotiation skills
  • Excellent analytical and conceptual skills, efficient and able to work independently by structuring the path forward
  • Driven and solutions- oriented personality, excelling in fast-paced, dynamic, and energetic settings, with strong team collaboration skills
  • Willingness to travel frequently and flexibility to accommodate travel requirements.
  • Excellent communication skills in English, German and further language skills are a plus
  • Very good knowledge of the Microsoft Office applications (Word, Excel and PowerPoint)
