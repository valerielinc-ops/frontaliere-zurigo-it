Responsabilità principali

  • YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
  • As part of our picking team, prepare customer orders by packing products into crates, organizing them on pallets, and deliver them to our customers nearby.
  • The picking process takes place in our CoolCell, at a temperature of 2–5°C.
  • On average, shifts last around 5 hours (sometimes shorter or longer, depending on workload).
  • Shifts run Sunday through Friday, with two teams (sometimes shorter or longer, depending on workload).
  • The delivery takes place on foot to the customers in a 10min radius from the office.
  • Shift Model: Initially, you will be scheduled for shifts within the following windows: Weekdays (12:00–22:00) and Sundays (09:00–22:00).
  • Mon – Fri Early: Starts at 12:00 (approx. 5h)
  • Mon – Fri Late: Starts at 14:00 (approx. 7h) Mon
  • Fri Delivery: Starts at 15:00 (approx. 6h)

  • Working in winter temperatures (2–5°C) while wearing warm gear (gloves/hat) is no problem for you. You can commit to at least:
  • 2 full weekdays per week (flexible regarding early/late shift).
  • 2 full Sundays per month (flexible regarding early/late shift).
  • Logistics or warehouse experience (picking, packing, pallet handling) is a plus, but not required.
  • You are a non-smoker.
