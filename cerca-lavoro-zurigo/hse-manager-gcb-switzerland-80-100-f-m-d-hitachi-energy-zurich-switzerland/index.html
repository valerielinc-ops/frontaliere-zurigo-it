The opportunity

Are you passionate about protecting people, safeguarding the environment, and driving a strong health and safety culture in a manufacturing environment?

As our HSE Manager for the Generator Circuit Breaker (GCB) factory in Switzerland, you will take full ownership of the Health, Safety, and Environment function and play a key role in shaping a safe, compliant, and sustainable workplace across our operations.

Processo di candidatura

  • Communicate HSE initiatives, programs, and best practices to ensure a consistent understanding and application across the organization
  • Monitor compliance with Swiss regulations, Hitachi Energy policies, and ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 requirements, ensuring timely resolution of non-conformances
  • Implement, maintain, and continuously improve the HSE Management System and associated governance processes
  • Lead HSE audits, inspections, and assurance activities while ensuring effective follow-up and closure of identified actions
  • Investigate incidents, near misses, and unsafe conditions, identify root causes, and drive sustainable corrective and preventive actions
  • Support factory operations, engineering projects, facility modifications, and investment initiatives through risk assessments and expert HSE guidance
  • Oversee contractor safety management and ensure effective risk controls for external service providers working on site
  • Lead HSE Reviews, monitor key performance indicators, and provide management with meaningful performance insights

  • Strong leadership personality with proven experience in leading teams and influencing stakeholders across all organizational levels
  • Sound understanding of occupational health, safety, environmental, and sustainability requirements within industrial operations
