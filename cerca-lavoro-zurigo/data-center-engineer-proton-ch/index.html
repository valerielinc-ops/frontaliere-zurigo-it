Descrizione completa

Join Proton and build a better internet where privacy is the default

At Proton, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right and the cornerstone of democracy. Since our inception in 2014, founded by a team of scientists from CERN, we have dedicated ourselves to providing free and open-source technology to millions worldwide, ensuring access to privacy, security, and freedom online.Our journey began with Proton Mail, the largest secure email service globally, and has since expanded to include Proton VPN, Proton Calendar, Proton Drive, and Proton Pass. These tools empower individuals and organizations to take control of their personal data, break away from Big Tech’s invasive practices, and defeat censorship. Our work impacts hundreds of millions of lives, from activists on the front lines defending freedom to leaders in governments protecting sensitive information. In some cases, Proton’s services have even been instrumental in saving lives by enabling secure and private communications in high-risk situations.

Proton is a profitable company that does not rely upon VC funding, supporting over 100 million user accounts with a growing team of over 500 people from over 50 different countries, from the world's top companies and universities. We value intelligence, learning potential, and ambition in our hiring process. Adaptability is key as we navigate uncharted territories and redefine how business is conducted online.

Hiring at Proton is highly selective, with less than 1% of candidates hired. We believe smaller teams of exceptional talent will always prevail over larger teams with lower talent density. You will have the opportunity work with many of the world's top minds in their fields, ranging from former international math and science olympiad winners to chess champions.

We have a global mindset and big ambitions but remain a start-up at heart. We value empowerment and flexibility and keep our structure flat to keep moving fast and avoid unnecessary politics. Tired of blending into the crowd? Join us and do work you can truly be proud of. Check our open-source projects here (proton.me/community/open-source)!

The team:

You will be joining the Data Center and Hardware Team.

At Proton, we operate our own private cloud infrastructure, which is distributed across multiple data centers and regions. This infrastructure, built primarily on open-source technology, is doubling every year and requires 24/7 operation.

Join our Team at Proton, where you will play a vital role in ensuring the smooth operation, scaling and maintenance of our private cloud infrastructure.

Purpose of the role

As a Data Center Engineer, you will be responsible for monitoring, troubleshooting, scaling and optimizing our infrastructure systems to ensure their reliability, cost efficiency and performance. You will collaborate closely with our IT team, network engineers, and system administrators to identify and resolve any issues, as well as propose and implement new innovations. You will work with cutting-edge infrastructure platforms and collaborate with a talented team of humans passionate about technology.

What you will do

● Server stacking and cabling installation in our rooms
● Performing HW troubleshooting and basic HW repairs for Servers and Network equipment (PSU, Network cards, CMOS batteries, Memory, Optics...)
● Diagnostic and repair of defective network fiber links and interfaces
● Coordinate logistics for new hardware and parts procurement
● Supervision Data Center remote hands contractors, work orders plan and execution for local and remote sites
● Management of material stock and purchases: Working together with our procurement teams to ensure all needed parts are available for the Data Center operations
● Data center environment monitoring
● Help run HW selection validation test, performance tuning and benchmarking as a pair of HW sourcing

Job requirements

● Experience racking and cabling servers with high standards
● Experience with HW troubleshooting
● Good understanding of Data Center infrastructure ( racks, cabling, power, cooling)
● Computer hardware architecture knowledge
● Basic user experience of configuration management tools and version control systems
● Ability to track and communicate the status of different projects
● Ability to keep up in an extremely fast-paced environment
● Good communication skills and ability to work in a multicultural environment
● Availability to travel to different locations for short-term projects
● Work proficiency in English

Bonus points for:

● Linux user or admin experience and scripting skills
● Advance HW repairs experience
● Project management experience
● DCIM experience as user or administrator
● HW provisioning, burn-in and validation experience
● Electrical engineer background
