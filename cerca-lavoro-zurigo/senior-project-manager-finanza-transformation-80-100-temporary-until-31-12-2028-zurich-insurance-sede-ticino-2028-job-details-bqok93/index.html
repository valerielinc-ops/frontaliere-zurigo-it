Descrizione completa

The Finance Transformation Senior Project Manager will support a multi year journey to fundamentally transform how we run, steer and enable our business. Across Zurich, we are simplifying processes, modernising our technology and empowering our people so we can make faster, better decisions and create a brighter future together.

As Senior Project Manager you will play a pivotal role in shaping and delivering this transformation. You will support complex, cross functional projects that cut across business units, functions and geographies, ensuring that strategy becomes reality and that our people are supported through the change. You will support stakeholders around a shared vision, bring structure and discipline to execution, and create the environment in which teams can succeed.

What you will do

  • Support finance transformation workstreams or teams ensuring clear scope, realistic plans, and delivery against agreed outcomes.
  • Support the program and workstream delivery roadmap planning working with workstream and functional leaders, aligning initiatives and timelines.
  • Enable governance and decision making forums, by ensuring timely, transparent status reporting, risk registers and project artefacts.
  • Coordinate cross functional workstreams (business, technology, finance, operations, change management) and manage interdependencies and risks.
  • Enable programme financials and benefits tracking, working closely with Finance to define business cases, track spend and measure value delivery.
  • Ensure monitoring and control of the project activities in accordance with the Zurich project management frameworks and policies.
  • Coordinate improvements in ways of working.
  • Support leadership teams with preparation of steering materials, executive updates, and key decision papers.
  • Help other colleagues to employ project management disciplines.

What you bring

  • Experience in projects or programmes in an international environment, ideally in financial services or a similarly regulated industry.
  • Ability to thrive in complex stakeholder environments, working with senior executives and diverse teams across functions and geographies.
  • Good organisational skills, including planning, governance, risk and issue management, financial tracking and benefits realisation.
  • Strong influencing and relationship building skills, with the ability to navigate ambiguity, build alignment and facilitate conversations.
  • Clear and compelling communication skills in English (written and verbal); additional languages are an advantage.
  • Relevant academic background, for example in Business, Finance, Economics, Technology, or a related field; professional certifications in project/programme management (e.g. PMP, MSP, SAFe) are an advantage.
  • A growth mindset and resilience, comfortable working in a fast moving, evolving environment and balancing multiple priorities.

You belong here.

Imagine working in one of the world’s most sustainable workplaces. At Quai Zurich Campus , you’ll enjoy an environment designed to help you thrive, with benefits that support your well-being and impact, and opportunities to perform at your best, surrounded by top talent and supportive colleagues.

You’ll have access to healthy food options, fitness facilities, and bright, collaborative spaces that spark creativity. Located in the heart of Zurich on the shores of Lake Zurich, the campus offers excellent public transportation links, making your commute simple.

Grow with us through access to learning resources, on-the-job development, and continuous empowerment and support. Be part of a team that is creating a brighter future together.

About Us

Zurich is a leading multi-line insurer, serving over 82 million customers in more than 200 countries and territories with more than 65,000 employees. For over 150 years, Zurich has been transforming insurance – offering not just protection, but also prevention services that promote well-being and climate resilience.

Guided by our purpose to ‘create a brighter future together,’ we aim to be one of the most responsible and impactful businesses in the world. We look forward to receiving your online application today!
