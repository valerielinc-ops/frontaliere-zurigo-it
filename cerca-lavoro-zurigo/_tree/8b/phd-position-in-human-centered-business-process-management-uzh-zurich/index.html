Descrizione completa

Department of Informatics

Start of employment 01.01.2027, temporary

The research group for Information Systems in Organizations (ISIO), led by Prof. Dr. Jana Rehse, conducts research at the intersection of business processes, information systems, and data analytics. We develop methods and technologies that help organizations understand, improve, and redesign the ways in which people, processes, and digital systems interact. By combining methods from computer science and information systems research with a deep understanding of organizational contexts, we seek to generate insights that are both scientifically novel and practically relevant. Our vision is to contribute to the development of intelligent, adaptive, and human-centered organizations.

Your responsibilities

We are looking for an excellent doctoral candidate who wants to conduct research on human-centered business process management. With the advent of AI, organizations are now faced with countless decisions on how to redesign their business processes and IT systems. The goal of this doctoral project is to provide them with guidance how to do this in a human-centric way, such that the processes improve the experiences of both employees and customers. Within this larger frame, you are welcome to co-develop your concrete topic such that it fits your background and interest as well as the group's research vision. You will also have the opportunity to cooperate with local organizations to see the impact of your research in practice.

Your profile

  • Master's degree in Business Informatics, Information Systems, Computer Science, Organizational Psychology or a related field
  • Strong interest in business process management, human-centered design, and organizational impact
  • High intrinsic motivation, independence, openness to new ideas, ability to collaborate, proactive communication
  • Proficiency in both English and German (written and spoken)
  • Experience in or willingness to learn design-oriented and empirical research methods
  • WIllingness to contribute to the establishment of the research group
