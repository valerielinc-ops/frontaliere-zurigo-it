Descrizione completa

Overview

As a Services Sales professional, you will lead strategic customer engagements that connect Microsoft’s consulting, support, cloud and AI capabilities with customers’ business priorities. You will identify and develop new opportunities, grow existing customer relationships, and help customers achieve measurable value through Microsoft solutions and services.

You will work closely with senior customer stakeholders, account teams, delivery teams and partners to develop account strategies, lead complex opportunities and negotiations, and support customers throughout the sales and delivery journey.

Microsoft’s mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. As employees we come together with a growth mindset, innovate to empower others, and collaborate to realize our shared goals. Each day we build on our values of respect, integrity, and accountability to create a culture of inclusion where everyone can thrive at work and beyond.

Responsibilities

  • Own and execute the consulting sales plan for large transformation opportunities across UKI and Europe South, building a repeatable pipeline aligned to strategic business priorities.
  • Connect strategic consulting and regional sales teams across UKI and Europe South, enabling alignment, knowledge sharing and coordinated execution across services, industry solutions and sales.
  • Identify, qualify, lead and close strategic consulting opportunities with senior customer stakeholders, driving cloud and AI adoption aligned to customer business value.
  • Partner with UKI and Europe South sales leadership to integrate transformation opportunities into account plans and support sellers in identifying, qualifying and advancing strategic engagements.
  • Represent UKI and Europe South in relevant sales planning and governance, maintaining accurate sales stages, forecasts and close plans.
  • Build and maintain senior stakeholder relationships across both markets, applying proven operating models to create a sustainable and repeatable approach to consulting-led cloud and AI growth

Qualifications

Required/Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree AND consultative sales experience in the commercial or public sector, such as finance or local government, or related work OR equivalent experience.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree in Business, Information Technology or a related field AND consultative sales experience in the commercial or public sector, such as finance or local government, or related work OR equivalent experience.
