Responsabilità principali

  • We are seeking a Technical Program Manager to drive process & tooling optimization and automation, primarily focused on AI driven improvements to accelerate transformation across global cloud and network operations.

  • The ideal candidate thrives in complex, matrixed environments, can move seamlessly between strategy and execution, and delivers measurable impact through responsible, scalable AI solutions. Key Responsibilities
  • Process Optimization, Tooling & Automation with Focus on AI
  • Map, analyze, and optimize end-to-end operational processes across network sourcing, inventory, planning, fiber lifecycle, and engineering workflows.
  • Eliminate friction, standardize practices, and reduce cycle times using AI enabled automation.
  • Partner with CIA and our partner teams to ensure integrated, scalable tooling solutions aligned to business priorities. AI, Data & System Integration
  • Strengthen data quality, interoperability, and automation across network and operational tools.
  • Enable AI powered data cleansing, reconciliation, forecasting, and predictive modeling.
