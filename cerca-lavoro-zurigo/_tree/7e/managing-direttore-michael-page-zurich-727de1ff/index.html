Descrizione completa

About Our Client Our client is a founder-led technology and marketplace business building a leading ecosystem across the DACH region within the travel, leisure, tourism, and digital services sectors.Headquartered in Zurich, the company is entering its next stage of growth and is seeking a successor to the founder who can lead the transition from an entrepreneurial start-up to a scalable up to an institutionally managed organization. The business combines a strong customer-facing platform with a broad network of commercial partners and service providers.We are looking for an entrepreneurial, hands-on CEO with a proven track record of building and scaling high-growth businesses. The successful candidate will define and execute a compelling B2B go-to-market strategy, accelerate commercial expansion, responsibility for all finance matters, and build the organizational capabilities required to support the company's ambitious growth trajectory. The role requires a true company builder who thrives in dynamic environments and can transform vision into sustainable scale

Job Description As a member of the senior leadership team, the Managing Director will contribute to the further development, professionalisation and expansion of the organisation. The position combines broad commercial leadership with responsibility for selected corporate and financial matters. Working closely with the main shareholder, the successful candidate will help translate the company's strategic ambitions into a scalable operating model.Key responsibilities will include:

  • Contributing to the definition and execution of the company's growth strategy.
  • Developing the organisation's commercial presence and routes to market in a Be2Be environment
  • Building and strengthening strategic relationships with commercial partners, service providers and other relevant stakeholders.
  • Structuring and scaling the company's partnership-management capabilities.
  • Supporting the development of new markets, channels and business opportunities.
  • Establishing clear commercial objectives, performance indicators and management processes.
  • Building financial planning, controlling, reporting and performance management.
  • Monitoring profitability, cash flow and the overall financial performance of the business.
  • Contributing to the ongoing development of governance, organisational and decision-making structures.
  • Supporting the coordination and performance management of activities across different markets.
  • Building and developing teams and leadership capabilities as the organisation grows.
  • Ensuring that commercial growth is supported by appropriate financial discipline and organizational structures.
  • Acting as a trusted sparring partner to the shareholder
  • Build and develop a lean team

The role will require a combination of strategic perspective and hands-on involvement. It offers the opportunity to influence the next stage of development of a business operating in a dynamic, consumer-oriented environment.

The Successful Applicant The successful candidate will be a builder with an entrepreneurial and commercially mindsete with the ability to operate across strategy, business development, finance and organisational leadership.

The profile should ideally include

  • Senior leadership experience as a Managing Director, CEO, Commercial Director, or commercial CFO or in a comparable scale-up environment
  • A successful track record in building, scaling a business to an institutional level, business unit or commercial organisation.
  • Relevant exposure to travel, tourism, hospitality, leisure, retail, consumer services, e-commerce, digital platforms or marketplace businesses .
  • Experience developing and managing B2B, B2B2C or partner-led commercial models.
  • Demonstrated ability to build relationships with independent commercial partners, distributors, agents, franchisees, affiliates or service providers.
  • Strong commercial judgement, including experience in business development, partner acquisition and the long-term development of partner performance.
  • Solid financial capabilities across planning, budgeting, controlling, reporting, liquidity management and profitability analysis.
  • Previous exposure to international, multi-site, decentralised or multi-entity organisations.
  • Strong analytical and strategic capabilities, combined with a pragmatic and execution-oriented approach.
  • Experience working with digital products, platforms or technology-enabled business models.
  • A leadership style characterised by integrity, resilience, sound judgement and a genuine willingness to build.
  • Only fluent German can be considered on top of very good command of English.
  • Willingness to travel regularly.

What's on Offer

  • A senior leadership position with meaningful influence on the future development of the organisation.
  • A broad mandate combining commercial growth, organisational development and financial leadership.
  • Equity stake for the entrepreneurial upside
