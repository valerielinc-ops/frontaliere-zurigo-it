About Our Client Our client is a leading international financial services organisation operating in a regulated environment and partnering with a wide range of institutional clients. Combining a collaborative culture, entrepreneurial mindset and strong international footprint, the company offers a high-visibility environment where individuals are empowered to influence strategic decisions and drive change.

Job Description

  • Lead and continuously enhance the compliance framework of the Swiss regulated entity.
  • Serve as the primary compliance contact for Swiss regulatory authorities, auditors and key external stakeholders.
  • Monitor regulatory developments and ensure timely implementation of applicable requirements.
  • Advise senior management and business leaders on regulatory, conduct and compliance matters.
  • Oversee AML, KYC and broader financial crime compliance activities.
  • Manage compliance monitoring, risk assessments and regulatory reporting processes.
  • Support regulatory reviews, audits and remediation programmes.
  • Contribute to strategic and business transformation initiatives.
  • Promote a strong culture of compliance across the organisation through close collaboration with Legal, Risk, Operations, HR and business teams.

The Successful Applicant We are seeking a broad and commercially minded compliance professional who can operate both strategically and operationally.

You are likely to bring

  • Experience as a Head of Compliance, Compliance Director, Chief Compliance Officer or a Deputy Head with significant autonomy.
  • Direct exposure to Swiss regulation, including interactions with FINMA, regulatory audits and governance bodies.
  • Experience within a regulated financial institution such as asset management, wealth management, private banking, securities, fund services, brokerage or fintech.
  • A generalist compliance background covering governance, regulatory reporting, monitoring, AML/KYC, financial crime and advisory activities.
  • The ability to operate independently and successfully within a lean, entrepreneurial environment.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills and the confidence to advise senior executives and board-level audiences.
  • Excellent communication skills in English. Additional languages are considered an asset.

What's on Offer

  • High visibility within a growing international organisation.
  • Significant autonomy and direct exposure to senior decision-makers.
  • A dynamic, agile and entrepreneurial environment.
  • Broad responsibilities across the entire compliance agenda rather than a narrow specialist mandate.
  • Opportunity to influence both local and regional compliance initiatives.
