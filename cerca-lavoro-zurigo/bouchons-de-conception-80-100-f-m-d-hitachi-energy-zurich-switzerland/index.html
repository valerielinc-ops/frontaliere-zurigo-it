Über die Position

Hitachi Energy, High Voltage Products, gehört zu den weltweit führenden Anbietern von Lösungen für die Energieübertragung und Energieverteilung.

Unsere Serviceorganisation stellt sicher, dass kritische Energieinfrastrukturen unserer Kunden rund um den Globus zuverlässig, sicher und effizient betrieben werden können.

Azienda e contesto

  • Our service organization ensures that our customers' critical energy infrastructure can be operated reliably, safely, and efficiently around the globe.
  • As a Technical Buyer, you will play a key role at the interface between Procurement, Engineering, Quality Assurance, and our global supplier network.
  • You will ensure that technically specified materials, components, and services meet the required quality, delivery, and cost targets while remaining available over the long term.
  • With your technical expertise and cross-functional collaboration, you will make an important contribution to product quality, supply security, and operational excellence.
  • In doing so, you will help Hitachi Energy successfully deliver innovative and sustainable energy solutions. How You'll Make an Impact
  • Clarify technical requirements related to materials, drawings, specifications, and engineering changes in close collaboration with Engineering and suppliers.
  • Support and coordinate PPAP (Production Part Approval Process) activities, initial sample inspections, and supplier qualification processes.
  • Conduct technical evaluations of alternative materials, deviation requests, and obsolescence cases.
  • Work closely with strategic and operational procurement teams on material- and supplier-related topics.
  • Support supplier claims, Non-Conformity Reports (NCRs), and root cause analyses.
