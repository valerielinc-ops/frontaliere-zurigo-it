Descrizione completa

As an Investor Relations Intern, you will collaborate closely with both the Investor Relations team and the CEO Office, gaining hands-on experience in fundraising, strategic decision-making, and corporate development. Working closely with experienced professionals, you will gain hands-on exposure to venture fundraising, capital markets and corporate strategy in a fast-growing digital asset bank. This internship offers the opportunity to work on high-impact projects, contributing to investor communications, operational excellence, and broader corporate initiatives. Responsibilities

  • Drive strategic initiatives: Take ownership of specific projects and initiatives, ensuring smooth execution and tangible impact.
  • Investor reporting & communication: Prepare and manage quarterly reports and investor updates, ensuring accuracy and timely delivery.
  • Fundraising & investor relations support: Assist in creating fundraising and investor relations presentations, participate in investor meetings, and manage follow-ups.
  • Market research & due diligence: Conduct in-depth market research and support due diligence efforts related to investment opportunities and strategic partnerships.
  • IPO & M&A support: Contribute to IPO and M&A-related processes, including research, documentation, and coordination with investment banks and other third parties.
  • Strategic projects: Work on key initiatives led by the CEO Office, such as international expansion, process improvements, and corporate strategy development.
  • AI & process innovation: Help design and implement new AI agents and tools to automate and streamline key processes, with the objective of increasing efficiency and quality across Investor Relations and the wider CEO Office.
  • You are currently completing or have recently completed a bachelor’s or master’s degree in business, finance, economics, engineering or a related discipline
  • You are an independent thinker who can execute tasks autonomously. We expect you to take the lead and drive things
  • You are an open-minded team player. You will work in a position where many endpoints come together. It is vital that you can efficiently collaborate with other teams to further push our goals as a company
  • A strong can-do attitude is essential. As a pioneer in the industry, your scope of action will be broad, and we expect you to be willing, and ideally enthusiastic, about tackling the challenges ahead. For some, you may already know the solution; for others, you will need to discover it
  • We do not expect extensive prior experience in every topic. However, we expect you to quickly understand unfamiliar subjects and build expertise independently
  • You have a genuine interest in digital assets and share our vision for the future of finance
  • You use AI tools effectively and responsibly, verify outputs and recognise the importance of confidentiality and data protection
  • You are proficient in PowerPoint and Excel, with the ability to communicate complex topics clearly, visually and with attention to detail
  • You communicate confidently and professionally, both verbally and in writing, in internal meetings and interactions with external partners
