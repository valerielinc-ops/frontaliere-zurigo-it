Descrizione completa

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

Are you passionate about financial markets, technology, and innovation? Are you eager to gain hands-on experience in securities lending and collateral management within a dynamic, forward-thinking private bank?

Join Julius Baer’s Securities Lending Team as an Intern and play a pivotal role in shaping the future of our trading operations.

You will collaborate closely with experienced traders and a wide range of internal stakeholders to maintain and improve solutions that enhance efficiency and client service excellence.

This is a unique opportunity to contribute meaningfully while building expertise in capital markets, collateral optimisation, and innovation.

We are looking for a motivated, analytically sharp, and tech-savvy student, who thrives in fast-paced environments and is excited to take ownership of real projects from day one.

If you are ready to immerse yourself in a challenging yet rewarding role at the intersection of finance and technology, we invite you to apply and become part of our journey.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • Play a central role in the automation of existing trading processes, taking ownership of individual projects
  • Trade Securities Lending Books and provide Short Covering for Clients and Bank
  • Provide overall support to the Securities Lending & Collateral Management Team
  • Building strong working relationships with internal teams, including understanding their daily processes

YOUR PROFILE

  • University degree in Computer Science, Finance, Economics or other STEM fields
  • Previous working experience in Finance, Data Science, or Engineering
  • Excellent programming skills in python, knowledge of VBA and SQL advantageous
  • Experience working on deep learning or machine learning projects will be considered an asset
  • Solid communication skills both verbally and written in English, German / French is a plus
  • Strong analytical skills, clear and structured thought processes
  • Creative thinker and comfortable taking initiative in pursuing new opportunities
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and changing priorities in a fast-paced environment
