Descrizione completa

At Zurich, our ambition is to put customers at the heart of everything we do and become the insurer of choice by building meaningful relationships that earn customer loyalty over time.

Over the past several years, we have made significant progress in building customer thinking into our strategy, products, services and ways of working. However, there is still more to do to ensure customer perspectives consistently shape decisions across the organisation and become embedded in our everyday mindset and culture. We believe better customer understanding leads to better business decisions.

The Group Customer Office plays a central role in this transformation, partnering with markets and functions around the world to ensure the voice of the customer is heard, understood and acted upon. Through customer, market and brand insights, we help the business better understand changing customer needs, identify opportunities for improvement and make decisions that strengthen customer loyalty, drive growth and create long-term value.

To support this effort, we are looking for a passionate, curious and commercially minded insights professional to join our Customer, Market & Brand Insights team. This role is paramount in translating our data and research into actionable insights that influence business decisions and drive meaningful change.

Working closely with markets, regional stakeholders and senior leaders, you will help evolve and deliver our global brand and experience insights tracking tools to deliver value to the business, ensuring customer understanding influences business decisions and actions.

As we continue to embrace AI and new analytical capabilities, we are also transforming how insight is generated and activated, allowing us to spend less time producing reports and more time influencing decisions and delivering impact.

Reporting to the Head of Global Brand Tracking & NPS, this role will focus on understanding how our brand health created through our products, services and experiences shapes customer perceptions and behaviours, and ensuring those insights are translated into actions that improve customer outcomes and business performance.

What you will do

As Senior Insight Manager you will

  • Champion the voice of the customer, helping embed customer thinking into decision-making across Zurich.
  • Generate compelling customer and brand insights that influence to drive business impact
  • Oversee Zurich's global retail Brand and Relationship NPS programmes and lead our Commercial Broker study, ensuring high-quality delivery, governance and business value.
  • Evolve our insight programmes and measurement approaches to ensure they remain relevant to changing customer and business needs.
  • Transform complex data into clear, actionable recommendations and executive-level narratives that drive action.
  • Partner with the insights team, markets, agencies and business stakeholders to identify opportunities, solve business challenges and accelerate customer-centred change.
  • Connect customer, brand and business data to strengthen understanding of performance drivers and commercial impact.
  • Facilitate discussions and workshops that turn insight into tangible business outcomes.
  • Work closely with the Tracking Insight Manager and TNPS Expert to maximise the impact, adoption and activation of insights across the organisation.
  • Bring external best practice, innovation and emerging AI capabilities into Zurich's insight ecosystem to continuously enhance how we generate and apply insight.

Work location is Zurich with travel potentially required up to 10%

What you bring

As Senior Insight Manager, you will bring

  • A passion for understanding customers and translating customer, market and brand data into meaningful business opportunities and actions.
  • Strong storytelling and communication skills, with the ability to distil complex information into clear, compelling narratives that influence decision-making.
  • Proven experience activating insights within organisations, driving awareness, engagement and action across diverse stakeholder groups.
  • Expertise in leading continuous and ad hoc qualitative and quantitative research programmes, either within an in-house insights function or consultancy environment.
  • Strong commercial acumen and the ability to connect customer insights with business performance, growth and loyalty outcomes.
  • Experience managing complex global programmes and building effective relationships across markets, functions and cultures.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence and engage senior stakeholders, facilitating discussions and workshops that drive alignment and action.
  • Curiosity and practical experience applying AI and emerging technologies to enhance insight generation, synthesis and activation.
  • Strong analytical skills with experience working with large datasets and statistical tools (e.g. Excel, SPSS), turning data into actionable recommendations.
  • Experience bringing together multiple data sources to identify performance drivers and generate deeper business understanding.
  • A degree (e.g. in business studies, marketing, research, psychology, behavioural science, customer or change management or a related field), alongside significant experience in customer, market or brand insights.
  • Knowledge of the insurance sector is beneficial but not essential; curiosity, commercial thinking and a customer-first mindset are critical.
  • Fluent English, with excellent written and verbal communication skills.
