Panoramica

Are you motivated by work that directly supports Zurich’s financial strength and resilience?

As a Senior Capital & Risk Analyst, you will help ensure that our Solvency II Standard Formula Capital Model meets regulatory requirements, supports sound solvency monitoring and risk-based decision-making.

In project activities, you will have the opportunity to drive improvements across our internal capital models and processes, take ownership of critical deliverables, and grow your expertise in a highly visible, Group-wide function.

Processo di candidatura

  • Act as subject matter expert to Zurich EU legal entities on the application of Solvency II standard formula calculations and regulatory submissions.
  • Monitoring of regulatory developments related to Solvency II standard formula methodology.
  • Ensure robust BAU operations for EU legal entities, together with Bratislava based Production- and Development Team.
  • Support projects and initiatives that enable continuous enhancements to Zurich’s internal capital model processes and its results. What you bring
  • Strong analytical background (University degree in Mathematics/Statistics/Finance, actuarial field or similar).
  • 3+ years of professional experience in insurance or related field.
  • Prior experience and knowledge in Solvency II and EIOPA regulation.
  • Good understanding of the financial and insurance capital and risk models.

Dettagli ulteriori

  • You will be a team member of quantitative analysts located in Zurich and Bratislava and will work in close collaboration with our EU legal entities and our in-house IT development team.
  • Support projects and initiatives that enable continuous enhancements to Zurich’s internal capital model processes and its results.
  • Be part of a team that is creating a brighter future together.
