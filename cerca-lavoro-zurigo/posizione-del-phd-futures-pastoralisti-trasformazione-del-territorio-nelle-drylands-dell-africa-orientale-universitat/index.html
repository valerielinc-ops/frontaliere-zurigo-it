Descrizione completa

Department of Geography

Start of employment January 2027 or upon agreement

The Political Geography Group at the Department of Geography, the University of Zurich, invites expressions of interest for the following PhD position. The PhD position is part of a Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF)-funded project, “Pastoralist Futures: Land Transformation in East Africa's Drylands”, which runs for four years. The position includes an SNSF-standard salary and entitlements to research funds for fieldwork in East Africa. The PhD position requires the candidate to write their dissertation on this theme, with a specific fieldwork focus on pastoralist territories in Southern Ethiopia and Northern Kenya. The project aims to understand the longue durée of pastoralist land transformation at the intersection of climate change, state intervention, and capitalist practices. It interrogates land-use and land-cover change across time and space, changes in property regimes, pastoralist populations' agency in navigating internally and externally driven changes, and practices of future-making.

Your responsibilities

The PhD candidate will develop a dissertation research project aligned with the project theme above and will be supervised by Dr. Asebe Regassa Debelo. You will join the Political Geography Group, which brings together researchers from Asia, Africa, and Europe to study the entanglements of development, humanitarianism, war, displacement, extraction, frontier and border violence, and environmental change. Alongside your PhD research, the position includes teaching responsibilities as required by the Graduate School in Geography and Earth System Sciences.

Your profile

You have or are close to completing a Master's degree in human geography, anthropology, development studies, area studies, or another related discipline In addition, research experience in East African pastoralist territories and language skills (Swahili and/or Afaan Oromo) will be an asset.
