Panoramica

Key responsibilities Do you have a strategic mindset to engage with senior stakeholders? Do you have strong problem solving skills and do you enjoy bringing structure to complex situations, engage with a variety of views and stakeholders to find common solutions? Are you good at communicating complex business challenges in a constructive and easy way?

  • holistically drive the development of the strategy for UBS Personal & Corporate Banking and Region Switzerland
  • support the decision making of our internal business partners on senior management level
  • conduct industry, competitor and benchmarking analyses
  • design operating models and processes for specific markets and business segments
  • prepare and coordinate business plans including financial modelling
  • develop implementation roadmaps of strategic initiatives
  • support M&A projects from a strategic perspective (rationale, financial opportunity, due diligence, etc.)
  • be open to do cross-divisional strategy projects/rotations as opportunities arise

The team You’ll be working in the Chief Strategy Office within Personal & Corporate Banking Strategy Team in Zurich. The Chief Strategy Office is a team of motivated professionals who bring a diverse international background. We are responsible for formulating and executing best in class vision and strategy, facilitating group and business level strategies and driving multi-functional projects and initiatives for the firm globally.

Your skills and experience

  • 1-4 years professional experience within a top management consulting firm or an equivalent in-house corporate development position, preferably with a focus on financial services
  • strong written, verbal and interpersonal communication skills to clearly articulate sophisticated concepts, ideas, and processes
  • entrepreneurial spirit who can work well cross-functionally in a diverse environment on multiple projects
  • independent thinking (constructively challenging the status quo) coupled with a proven ability to work with others and generate impact in a team environment
  • well-developed strategic thinking ability with commercial market awareness and business acumen
  • disciplined organization and topic/project management skills
  • high level of energy, initiative, discipline, tenacity, and self-motivation
  • highest level of integrity and professionalism
  • a good command of the German language is required
  • knowledge of the Swiss economy and market is a plus
