About the job Google's software engineers develop the next-generation technologies that change how billions of users connect, explore, and interact with information and one another. Our products need to handle information at massive scale, and extend well beyond web search. We're looking for engineers who bring fresh ideas from all areas, including information retrieval, distributed computing, large-scale system design, networking and data storage, security, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, UI design and mobile; the list goes on and is growing every day. As a software engineer, you will work on a specific project critical to Google’s needs with opportunities to switch teams and projects as you and our fast-paced business grow and evolve. We need our engineers to be versatile, display leadership qualities and be enthusiastic to take on new problems across the full-stack as we continue to push technology forward.

The Business Agent team sits at the core of conversational AI empowering Google's Agentic Commerce goal. We are building the authoritative, AI-powered representation of businesses, enabling consumers to interact seamlessly through intuitive, action-oriented interfaces on Search, YouTube, Ads, and Messaging (e.g., Rich Communication Services (RCS)) with Business Agent. Our efforts are focused on turning conversational interactions into commerce outcomes. We are building the scalable platform and distributed infrastructure required to serve millions of merchants and consumers. We focus on solving complex tests around low-latency orchestration, ensuring that multi-turn AI reasoning loops and tool-calling pipelines remain fast and responsive. We manage data ingestion into the Merchant Knowledge Library, building pipelines that structure proprietary business data to optimize RAG. We build continuous automated evaluation pipelines and specialized AutoRaters.People shop on Google more than a billion times a day
