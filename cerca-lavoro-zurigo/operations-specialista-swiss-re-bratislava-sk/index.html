About the Role

Are you passionate about driving operations and strategic initiatives within a dynamic global team? Join us as our new Operations Specialist and play a pivotal role in shaping the future of Data & Technology Finance (D&T Finance).

Your role and responsibilities include

  • Manage divisional operations processes in alignment with Data & Technology strategic priorities
  • Create, compile, and present management reports, presentations, and workforce data analyses
  • Oversee and execute strategic workforce planning in line with financial targets
  • Ensure adherence to the governance framework and support consistent operational standards
  • Oversee Objectives & Key Results and manage quarterly review processes
  • Prepare, coordinate, and facilitate leadership meetings and divisional events
  • Act as a trusted partner and central point of contact for operational topics across the leadership team and wider division

You will directly contribute to strategic decision-making and drive operational excellence across the division.

About the Team

We are the central D&T Finance Office, currently a team of four, embedded within a larger Tech Division of approximately 400 internal employees delivering technology capabilities for Swiss Re’s Finance function . As a globally present team, you will collaborate with colleagues across locations and time zones. We are responsible for strategic and financial oversight, communication strategy and execution, and key operational processes across the division.

About You

  • Strategic and creative thinker with high energy, proactivity, and a strong can-do attitude
  • Experience in operations management, project management, consulting, or similar fields, ideally with at least 5 years of relevant experience
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with confidence engaging with leadership teams
  • Proficient in MS Office / Office 365 tools, including Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Teams, SharePoint, and Loop
  • Experienced in working with data, deriving insights, and using tools such as dashboards, Excel pivot tables, or similar reporting solutions
  • Experienced in, or motivated to learn, how emerging AI and automation tools can improve operational efficiency and ways of working
