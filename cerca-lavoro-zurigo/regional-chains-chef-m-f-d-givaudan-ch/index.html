Regional Chains Chef (m/f/d)

Overall mission

Guide the successful execution of culinary innovation projects for Food Service and QSR customers by transforming consumer insights and our priorities into scalable, customer-focused menu solutions.

You will be based in Kemptthal and report to the Senior Business Development Chef.

You will

  • Support the Senior Business Development Chef and the culinary team in accomplishing the Food Service and QSR strategy through customer-focused culinary projects.
  • Align daily activities with commercial and innovation priorities, contributing to business growth and customer satisfaction.
  • Translate our PPA into menu concepts that showcase flavour, texture and sensory leadership.
  • Contribute to trend scouting, concept development and innovation initiatives relevant to QSR and foodservice operators.
  • Develop prototypes, tastings and culinary showcases that bring innovation to life through consumer-relevant experiences.
  • Collaborate with Marketing, Commercial, FC&A and Technical teams to transform concepts into scalable menu solutions.
  • Support customer engagement through compelling culinary storytelling, presentations and sensory experiences.
  • Promote co-creation activities and contribute to project inflow and pipeline development.
  • Coordinate project logistics, workshops, tastings and customer visits, ensuring and high-quality execution.
  • Maintain an organised kitchen and lab environment, ensuring efficient workflows, traceability and compliance.
  • Use digital tools to manage recipes, samples, costings and sensory documentation.
  • Apply an understanding of commercial considerations when developing culinary recommendations.
  • Support continuous improvement through documentation, feedback collection and knowledge sharing.
  • Build expertise in market trends, menu inspiration and category insights to support reactive and proactive innovation.
  • Foster collaboration across regional and global culinary, commercial, marketing, product development and operations teams.
  • Demonstrate curiosity and teamwork while contributing to a culture of culinary excellence.

You

  • Culinary or food engineering qualification.
  • 3+ years of experience in Food Service, QSR, hospitality, or the food industry.
  • Experience with culinary innovation and consumer trends.
  • Experience in recipe, menu or concept development.
  • Collaborative attitude across departments and with customers.
  • Experience with food safety and quality standards.
  • Fluency in English
  • Willingness to travel across the region occasionally.
