Descrizione completa

As Data Engineer within Finance Platform, you will play a key role in connecting our Finance applications with our modern data landscape. You will take ownership of integrating Finance data into Snowflake, contribute to the further development of our Finance Data Marts, and help ensure reliable, high-quality data for Accounting, Controlling and other Finance use cases. Combining data engineering with an understanding of Finance applications and processes, you will work closely with business and technology stakeholders to shape sustainable, scalable data solutions.

What would your role be? As part of the Finance Platform team, you will have the opportunity to shape and further develop our Finance data landscape and its integration with our core Finance applications.

In this role, you will

  • Take ownership of the Finance Data Marts and their underlying data models, contributing to their continuous development and evolution.
  • Design, build and maintain reliable data pipelines from Finance applications into Snowflake.
  • Work with Finance applications and their underlying data, combining application understanding with hands-on data engineering.
  • Ensure high standards of data quality, reliability and availability across Finance data flows.
  • Translate requirements from Accounting, Controlling and other Finance stakeholders into scalable technical data solutions.
  • Collaborate closely with Finance, application teams, data specialists and other technology stakeholders across the organization.
  • Support the ongoing modernization of the Finance Platform by helping establish sustainable and scalable data architectures and integration patterns.
  • Contribute to the operational stability of the Finance application landscape while driving its evolution toward a more data-driven Finance environment.

What are we looking for?

  • A university degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.
  • Several years of experience in Data Engineering, Data Warehousing or a comparable engineering role.
  • Strong SQL skills and hands-on experience designing, building and maintaining data pipelines.
  • Practical experience with Snowflake and modern data platforms.
  • Programming experience in Python.
  • Solid understanding of data modelling principles, including Data Vault modelling, ETL/ELT concepts and data integration patterns.
  • The ability to understand Finance processes and application landscapes and translate business requirements into pragmatic data solutions.
  • Experience integrating data from enterprise applications, ideally SAP or other Finance systems.
  • Experience with Microsoft Azure, dbt, CI/CD, automation or modern DevOps practices is an advantage.
  • Experience in banking, financial services, Accounting or Controlling is an advantage.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a high awareness of data quality, reliability and maintainability.
  • Excellent communication skills in English
  • German is an advantage.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.
  • A hands-on engineer who enjoys taking ownership and bringing solutions into production.
  • Curious about how applications, processes and data fit together end to end.
  • Comfortable working independently while collaborating closely with business and technology stakeholders.
  • Pragmatic and solution-oriented, balancing engineering quality with business needs.
  • Motivated to build sustainable, scalable solutions rather than short-term fixes.
  • Proactive in challenging existing approaches and continuously improving the way we work.
  • Able to navigate between application topics and data engineering rather than seeing them as separate worlds.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels.
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit.

We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes. Even if you do not have absolutely everything we have asked for, if this role sounds like a good fit for you, we encourage you to apply.
