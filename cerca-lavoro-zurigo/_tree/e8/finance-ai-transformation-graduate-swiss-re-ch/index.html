Descrizione completa

Start your journey towards a rewarding career! We’re looking for curious, motivated graduates with strong people skills who want to grow, learn, and make a difference. If you hold a Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD and have up to three years of professional experience, apply for our 18-month Graduate Programme starting on 1 September 2027. You’ll gain hands-on experience in the fast-paced world of reinsurance, rotate into different local teams, and receive tailored training to build your expertise. This is your opportunity to shape your career, help protect societies from the world’s most threatening hazards, and contribute to making the world more resilient. By the end of the programme, you’ll have built the skills, foundations and network to pursue a wide range of career opportunities within Swiss Re. About the Role

AI is changing how finance works, and you can help shape how that happens at Swiss Re. In this role, you'll join Global Finance Operations & Transformation (GFO&T) at a key moment in Finance's AI transformation. You'll help turn new technology into practical solutions that make our global finance operations faster, smarter and more reliable. You'll work with finance experts, actuaries, data specialists and technology teams, and see first-hand how a global reinsurer uses AI to make better decisions.

  • Find opportunities to use AI, automation and analytics across global finance operations such as reporting, closing and analysis
  • Help design, test and roll out AI-powered tools and prototypes, from first idea to real business use
  • Turn complex financial and actuarial data into clear insights that help stakeholders make decisions
  • Keep up with the latest AI trends and share ideas on how GFO&T can use them responsibly
  • Help colleagues across Finance adopt new tools and ways of working, so that change sticks

About the Team

You'll be part of a team that sits where finance and technology meet, with a shared goal of building the finance function of the future. We are a diverse, collaborative group of finance professionals, actuaries, data experts and change-makers. We like to experiment, learn quickly and share what we find. Ideas are welcome from everyone, whatever their seniority, and our leaders give graduates real responsibility from day one, with the guidance and support to succeed. Our work has visible impact across Swiss Re. It helps the business get insights faster, work more efficiently and decide with more confidence. You'll also learn from colleagues who are passionate about using AI to make a real difference. Through your rotation(s), you'll have the opportunity to work in other area(s) and build a strong network.

About You

You have a can-do attitude and a real enthusiasm for AI and what it can achieve. You're curious, proactive and happy to roll up your sleeves to solve problems, even when the path isn't clearly mapped out. You communicate clearly, enjoy working with others and can explain technical ideas to non-technical colleagues. Above all, you're excited to be part of a transformation and to keep learning as technology changes.

Essential Criteria

  • University degree (Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD) in Actuarial Science, Data Science, Data Engineering, Finance, or a related quantitative field, with up to 3 years of professional experience
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret complex information
  • A genuine interest in AI, with a good understanding of current trends such as generative AI, large language models and automation
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with confidence working across different stakeholders
  • Strong written and spoken English skills

Nice to Have

  • Hands-on experience with programming or data tools such as Python, SQL, R or Power BI
  • Experience building or experimenting with AI or machine learning solutions, for example through university projects, hackathons or personal projects
  • Understanding of financial or actuarial concepts in insurance, such as financial reporting or IFRS 17
  • Previous internship or relevant experience in insurance, financial services or a digital transformation setting
