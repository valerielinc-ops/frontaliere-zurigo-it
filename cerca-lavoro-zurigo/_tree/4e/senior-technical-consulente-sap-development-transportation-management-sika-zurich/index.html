Descrizione completa

About the Role We are looking for an experienced and technically proficient SAP Technical Project Manager to lead and coordinate SAP technology initiatives across our global SAP landscape. The ideal candidate combines a solid technical background in at least one of the core SAP technology domains — SAP Basis, SAP Development, or SAP Integration — with strong project management, communication, and organizational capabilities. Key Responsibilities Technical Project Management

  • Lead end-to-end SAP technical projects, from initiation and planning through implementation, testing, go-live, and hypercare.
  • Define project scope, deliverables, milestones, resource requirements, dependencies, and timelines.
  • Coordinate technical activities across SAP Basis, Development, Integration, Security, Infrastructure, and other relevant IT teams.
  • Manage project risks, issues, dependencies, and escalations, proactively identifying potential delivery obstacles.
  • Ensure technical solutions are aligned with enterprise architecture, IT security standards, and SAP best practices.
  • Monitor project progress, communicate status to stakeholders, and ensure timely delivery within agreed scope, quality, and budget.
  • Organize technical workshops, project meetings, and decision-making sessions with internal and external stakeholders.

SAP Technology Coordination

  • Coordinate technical initiatives involving SAP S/4HANA, SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), and connected SAP and non-SAP solutions.
  • Lead and implement new technology platforms in the areas of logistics, transportation and supply chain, such as BN4L, GTT
  • Manage technical activities related to SAP upgrades, migrations, system transformations, infrastructure modernization, and cloud adoption.
  • Support integration projects involving SAP Integration Suite, APIs, middleware, and interfaces between enterprise applications.
  • Coordinate SAP development initiatives involving ABAP, enhancements, custom applications, and technical remediation.
  • Oversee technical readiness activities, including environment preparation, technical testing, cutover planning, deployment, and production stabilization.
  • Understand technical dependencies between SAP systems, integrations, applications, and infrastructure components.
  • Facilitate technical discussions and ensure that proposed solutions are feasible, supportable, secure, and aligned with the overall SAP strategy.

Stakeholder and Vendor Management

  • Act as the central coordination point between technical teams, functional teams, business stakeholders, and external partners.
  • Translate technical challenges into clear business impacts, risks, and recommendations.
  • Coordinate internal resources, external consultants, and implementation partners across multiple countries and time zones.
  • Ensure effective communication, accountability, and transparency throughout project execution.
  • Manage technical escalations and facilitate timely resolution of cross-team dependencies.
  • Collaborate with SAP architects, technical leads, and service owners to support architectural and implementation decisions.

Governance, Quality and Operational Readiness

  • Ensure projects follow established IT governance, change management, documentation, and security processes.
  • Coordinate technical design reviews, testing strategies, implementation approvals, and go-live readiness assessments.
  • Ensure adequate knowledge transfer, technical documentation, monitoring, and operational handover to support teams.
  • Identify opportunities for standardization, automation, technical simplification, and continuous improvement.
  • Contribute to the development and execution of the SAP technology roadmap.

Your Skills and Experience

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.
  • Several years of professional experience working in SAP technical environments, combined with demonstrated experience coordinating or managing IT projects.
  • Strong hands-on technical background, current or previous, in at least one of the following areas:
  • SAP Basis: SAP system administration, architecture, upgrades, migrations, system landscapes, SAP HANA, or technical operations.
  • SAP Development: ABAP development, SAP extensibility, custom applications, enhancements, or technical development architecture.
  • SAP Integration: SAP Integration Suite, SAP Process Orchestration, APIs, middleware, interfaces, or enterprise integration architecture.
  • Good understanding of SAP system landscapes, technical dependencies, and the interaction between SAP applications and infrastructure.
  • Proven ability to plan, organize, coordinate, and deliver complex technical initiatives involving multiple teams.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to understand technical challenges and facilitate practical solutions.
  • Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and presentation skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a global, multicultural environment with distributed teams.
  • Strong organizational skills, ownership mindset, and ability to manage competing priorities.
  • Fluent English, both written and spoken.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA technical upgrades, migrations, or transformation programs.
  • Familiarity with SAP BTP, SAP Integration Suite, SAP Cloud ALM, and modern SAP cloud architectures.
  • Exposure to SAP RISE with SAP, hybrid landscapes, or cloud migration projects.
  • Knowledge of SAP technical change management, transport management, release management, and deployment processes.
  • Familiarity with ITIL, Agile, Scrum, or traditional project management methodologies.
  • Project management certification such as PMP, PRINCE2, or equivalent is an advantage.
  • Experience coordinating external service providers, system integrators, and managed service partners.
  • Knowledge of IT security, authorization concepts, and technical governance.
  • German language skills are an advantage.

Personal Competencies

  • Technically credible: Comfortable discussing SAP technical subjects with specialists and understanding the implications of proposed solutions.
  • Delivery-oriented: Focused on results, accountability, deadlines, and successful project execution.
  • Proactive and structured: Able to anticipate risks, establish priorities, and drive activities without constant supervision.
  • Strong communicator: Capable of adapting communication to both technical experts and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Collaborative leader: Able to coordinate and motivate multidisciplinary teams without necessarily having direct line-management authority.
  • Pragmatic problem-solver: Able to balance technical quality, business requirements, timelines, and available resources.
  • Adaptable and curious: Interested in evolving SAP technologies, cloud platforms, automation, and emerging digital capabilities.

Why Join Us

  • The opportunity to manage impactful SAP technology initiatives within a global enterprise environment.
  • Exposure to a diverse SAP landscape, including S/4HANA, SAP BTP, enterprise integration, and cloud transformation technologies.
  • Collaboration with experienced international SAP teams and technology partners.
  • A role combining technical depth, project leadership, and strategic technology transformation.
  • Opportunities for professional development and involvement in innovative SAP initiatives.

We offer competitive salaries, aligned with local market benchmarks and the specific scope and responsibilities of each role. Compensation is determined based skills relevant to the position, education and/or training. We are committed to fair and equitable pay practices in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemicals company with a globally leading position in the development and production of systems and products for bonding, sealing, damping, reinforcing, and protection in the building sector and industrial manufacturing. Sika has subsidiaries in 103 countries around the world and, in over 400 factories, produces innovative technologies for customers worldwide. In doing so, it plays a crucial role in enabling the transformation of the construction and transportation sector toward greater environmental compatibility. With more than 33,000 employees, the company generated sales of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
