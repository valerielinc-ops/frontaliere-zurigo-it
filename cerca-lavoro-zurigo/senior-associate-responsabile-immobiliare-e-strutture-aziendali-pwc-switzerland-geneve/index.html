Dein Team

You will become part of our Corporate Real Estate Management Team (CREM) at PwC Switzerland. The team is responsible for the strategic and operational management of the entire real estate portfolio – ranging from tenant fit-out projects and facility management to the coordination of external service providers. Working closely with internal stakeholders, owners, and authorities, CREM creates modern, safe and sustainable working environments where employees feel comfortable and can fully realise their potential. Dein Impact

  • Management of the PwC real estate portfolio in the Romandie region (Geneva, Lausanne and Neuchâtel), with a focus on ensuring smooth and efficient operations
  • Coordination and supervision of external service and facility management providers, including quality control and documentation
  • Identification of user needs, advising internal stakeholders and developing practical, customer‑oriented solutions
  • End‑to‑end responsibility for the new construction project in Geneva – covering both construction and operational aspects
  • Independent driving and implementation of sustainability initiatives within the real estate and facility management context
  • Responsibility for budget planning, cost control and adherence for the assigned PwC locations
  • Ensuring contract and rent management, including coordination with property management and review of service charge statements
  • Maintenance and implementation of site‑specific emergency and evacuation concepts
  • Organisation and execution of emergency and evacuation drills (approx. 6–8 per year) in close collaboration with Corporate Security
  • Preparation of analyses, presentations and reports
  • Active contribution to the continuous improvement of processes, standards and ways of working within the CREM environment

Dein Skill Set

  • Several years of professional experience (approx. 2–4 years) in real estate management or facility management, ideally in a corporate environment
  • Qualification as a certified real estate manager, a Bachelor's degree in Facility Management, or a comparable qualification is an advantage
  • Interest in or initial practical experience with construction, refurbishment or tenant fit‑out projects
  • Fluent French skills (native level or C1), good English skills
  • German is an advantage
  • Confident handling of MS 365; experience with real estate or CAFM systems such as Planon or Spacewell is a plus
  • Communicative, service‑oriented and proactive personality with a structured way of working and a solution‑oriented mindset
