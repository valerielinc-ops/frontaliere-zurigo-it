Your Team

In our Audit - Asset Management practice, we go beyond numbers. We work with leading private equity and asset management firms, helping them navigate complex financial reporting requirements, investment structures and regulatory developments. Our international team combines industry expertise with digital audit tools to deliver insights that build trust among investors and stakeholders. Whether auditing fund structures, assessing investment valuations or coordinating cross-border engagements, you will have the opportunity to expand your sector knowledge, take ownership and develop as a trusted audit professional. Your Impact

  • Take ownership of the planning, execution and completion of financial statement audits for asset managers, private equity firms and investment funds.
  • Audit complex accounting and valuation areas, including fair value measurements, investment valuations, impairment models and financial instruments.
  • Coordinate international engagement teams and service delivery centers across different locations, ensuring effective collaboration, consistent quality and timely execution.
  • Build trusted relationships with clients and support them with questions relating to IFRS, US GAAP and other relevant financial reporting requirements.
  • Apply data analytics and digital audit tools to identify risks, improve audit quality and make engagement delivery more efficient.
  • Coach junior colleagues through clear delegation, regular feedback and practical on-the-job guidance.

Your Skill Set

  • At least three busy seasons of external audit experience, gained within an international or complex client environment.
  • Completed or advanced professional audit qualification such as ACA, ACCA, Swiss CPA, US CPA or an equivalent certification.
  • Sound knowledge of IFRS and/or US GAAP, with practical experience auditing judgemental accounting areas and financial models.
  • Experience coordinating audit workstreams across international teams and service delivery centers and managing multiple stakeholders.
  • Interest in developing expertise in asset management, private equity, private markets or investment funds; prior sector experience is an advantage.
  • Excellent communication skills in English
  • German language skills and knowledge of Swiss or FINMA requirements are an advantage
