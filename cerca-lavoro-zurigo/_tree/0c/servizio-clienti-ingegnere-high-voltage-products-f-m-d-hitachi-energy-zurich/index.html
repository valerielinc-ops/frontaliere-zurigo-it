Descrizione completa

The opportunity

Our local product group Insulation & Components is the technology leader in design and manufacture of high voltage transformer bushings. All renowned international companies of the electrical industry, active in power generation, transmission & distribution are among our customers. We have a top position in the high voltage bushing areas worldwide. Bring your passion, bring your energy, and plug into an innovative and dynamic team.

Are you a flexible and proactive personality with good negotiation and communication skills, a hands-on mentality and pragmatism? Do you enjoy working with customers and leading investigations of field failures with a high degree of self-motivation and the ability to set the right priorities? Are you also a confident team player, competent, resilient, and with a high level of internal and external customer orientation?

We are looking forward to receiving your application.

How you'll make an impact

  • Advise our customers world-wide in solving technical challenges
  • Independently manage customer service cases in a structured way to define necessary actions and if necessary, lead the team to perform analysis according to lean methodologies (DMAIC, 8D, A3, etc.)
  • Conduct and manage root-cause analysis investigations to prevent avoidable problems from reoccurring
  • Prepare service-related quotation to enhance service sales and customer satisfaction, and execute order processes for spare parts and repairs
  • Coordinate on-site operations and maintenance
  • Prepare and conduct training for our customers, sales specialists and other stakeholders
  • Establish and maintain a close relationship with our customers and fulfill customer inquiries

Your background

  • Electrical engineering background, or similar education
  • Knowledge and experience with high voltage bushings, transformers, power grids, energy systems
  • Experience in dealing with customers
  • Prior experience in a similar role
  • First exposure to root-cause analysis methodologies (DMAIC, 8D, A3, etc.)
  • Exceptional problem-solving skills paired with excellent communication skills
  • Reliable and independent personality with a high degree of initiative, responsibility and commitment
  • Fluent in English, intermediate in German
