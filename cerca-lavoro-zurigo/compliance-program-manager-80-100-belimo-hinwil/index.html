Responsabilità principali

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Lead and coordinate the enterprise Compliance Management Framework, including governance, accountability models, RACI structures, and integration into the Integrated Management System (IMS).
  • Drive cross-functional compliance governance and foster collaboration with Compliance Category Owners and key stakeholders across business functions and regions.
  • Monitor regulatory developments, maintain the Compliance Radar and Dashboard, and provide compliance insights and updates for management reviews.
  • Manage the risk-based, multi-year integrated compliance audit program across functions and global locations.
  • Coordinate audit preparation, scheduling, execution support, documentation, reporting, and collaboration with internal audit teams.
  • Track compliance findings, corrective actions, deadlines, and escalation of overdue actions through a centralized compliance process.
  • Coordinate updates to IMS processes, roles and responsibilities based on regulatory changes, audit findings, compliance gaps, and identified improvement opportunities while promoting best practice sharing.
  • Prepare compliance reports, facilitate biannual compliance reviews, and support the implementation and transition of the integrated compliance framework into business-as-usual operations. Required Expertise
