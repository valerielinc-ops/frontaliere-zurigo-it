The Opportunity At Hitachi Energy, we're accelerating the energy transition by building a more sustainable, flexible and secure energy system.

As our Global Tax Operations Lead , you will have a unique opportunity to develop, lead and strengthen a recently defined global tax function that serves as the operational backbone of our Tax organisation.

Azienda e contesto

  • How You'll Make an Impact Lead and strengthen the global Tax Operations function, enhancing its operating model, governance framework, and organizational effectiveness.
  • Drive the execution of consistent, accurate and compliant end-to-end transactional tax processes, thrive continuous improvement in tax compliance, data quality, and reporting effectiveness across all regions.
  • Drive large-scale process transitions, integrations and stabilisation activities while maintaining business continuity throughout transformation programmes.
  • Standardise and optimise tax compliance operations globally, identifying opportunities to improve efficiency, quality, controls and scalability.
  • Set and monitor KPIs and quality standards to ensure effective and efficient execution of the tax strategy.
  • Develop a high-performing global team, ensuring effective workforce planning, capability development and resource allocation.
  • Coordinate responses to tax authority audits and inquiries in alignment with the Global Tax Strategy and internal stakeholders.
  • Act as the main escalation point for tax compliance issues and drive timely, sustainable resolution working with tax managers and practice team leaders.
  • Partner closely with tax, finance, IT, legal, supply chain and regional\/local business stakeholders to ensure operational delivery remains aligned with global requirements and business objectives.
  • Your Background Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Finance, Accounting, Tax, Business Administration or a related field. 15+ years of experience in global tax functions, tax process management, shared services, or similar tax operations environments.
