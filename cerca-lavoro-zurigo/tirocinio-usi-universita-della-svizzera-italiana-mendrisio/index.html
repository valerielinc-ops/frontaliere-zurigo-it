  • You are passionate about sustainable architecture, environmental technologies, and experimental design.
  • You enjoy combining analytical thinking with hands-on making and are motivated by solving complex interdisciplinary challenges.

Requisiti principali

  • You are passionate about sustainable architecture, environmental technologies, and experimental design.
  • You enjoy combining analytical thinking with hands-on making and are motivated by solving complex interdisciplinary challenges.
  • We are looking for candidates with the following profile:
  • Currently enrolled in, or recently graduated from, a Bachelor's or master’s programme in Architecture, Architectural Engineering, Landscape Architecture, Environmental Design, Civil Engineering, Computational Design, or a related field.
  • Interest in sustainable construction, façade systems, green infrastructure, and nature-based solutions.
  • Experience with CAD and digital modelling software (Rhino, Grasshopper, Revit or similar).
  • Familiarity with computational design, digital fabrication, prototyping, or environmental simulation is an advantage.
  • Comfortable working both digitally and physically through model making, prototyping, and experimental testing.
  • Strong curiosity, initiative, and willingness to work within an interdisciplinary research team.
