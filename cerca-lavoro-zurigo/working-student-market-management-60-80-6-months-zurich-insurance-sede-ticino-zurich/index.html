Descrizione completa

At Zurich, we are embedding the Customer into the heart of everything we do to represent them as best we can. As such we have a new role in our critical area of Commercial Insurance and we are seeking a Market Management Specialist who will be working in the central Commercial Insurance Market Management Team, which is part of the global Customer, Distribution and Market Management function. The team strives for impact by working closely with global and local teams and functions, as well as by blending hands-on marketing activation with thought leadership and research.

What you will do

  • Coordinate, support, plan and execute our global customer R NPS project (and any other customer or broker research initiatives) for Commercial Insurance, this implies:
  • Coordinate the rollout of the studies together with the Global Customer Office insights function
  • In cooperation with the responsible in the countries deliver the information and data necessary for the roll-out
  • Provide feedback on the questionnaires / surveys
  • Lead the internal communication process for the whole project for Commercial Insurance
  • Monitor the roll-out together with the Global Customer Office insights function
  • Troubleshooting during fieldwork
  • Turn findings into action, together with global and local colleagues
  • Support budget, invoice and supplier tracking

These next activities matter just as much

  • Run and improve our quarterly market management reporting
  • Coordinate internal and external events and webinars - both logistics and content
  • Help create and distribute our risk insights content
  • Shape the content plan for the Commercial Insurance LinkedIn channel
  • Contribute to and lead distribution and sales enablement projects linked to marketing, such as our LinkedIn advocacy programme
  • Take on ad-hoc projects as they arise

What you will bring

  • Excellent English proficiency, presentation and communication skills
  • Analytic and problem-solving skills
  • Strong interest in project management (global projects with a multitude of stakeholders)
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office package
  • Team orientation and ability to work effectively with all / different levels in the organization
  • Interests across several areas (marketing, analytics, insights, project management)
  • Curiosity and motivation to simplify and innovate and challenge the status quo
