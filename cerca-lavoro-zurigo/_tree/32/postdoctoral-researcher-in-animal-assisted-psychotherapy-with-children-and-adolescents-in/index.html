Department of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy, University Hospital of Psychiatry Zurich

Start of employment January 1st, 2027, temporary

We are recruiting a postdoctoral researcher to join the Department of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (KJPP) of the University Hospital of Psychiatry Zurich (PUK). The position is part of the interdisciplinary project “Meeting at eye level: Innovation in animal-assisted child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy”, a collaboration between the KJPP Zurich and the Messerli Research Institute in Vienna, Austria. You will join an interdisciplinary and practice-oriented research team combining expertise in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy, animal ethics, cognitive biology and animal welfare science across two universities and a psychiatric clinic. The KJPP Zurich offers animal-assisted therapies at several sites, ranging from direct interactions with dogs to caring for and observing farm animals. These activities will be further expanded and supplemented by innovative therapy approaches in a new therapy house for particularly vulnerable patients (mainly with autism spectrum disorders or refugee experience). The position offers opportunities for independent research, scientific exchange and the supervision of doctoral research, as well as close collaboration with clinical services and the animal ethics and cognitive biology research teams in Vienna through regular online meetings and research visits. You will contribute to an international interdisciplinary research project, with opportunities for scientific development and collaboration between research and clinical practice. We offer a funded postdoc position (90%, 3 years) at the PUK Zurich with a yearly salary of CHF 108'000.-. Start date is January 1st, 2027. The application deadline for this position is December 20th, 2026. University Hospital of Psychiatry Zurich Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Neumünsterallee 9 8008 Zurich Hosting project The advertised position is hosted by the project „Meeting at eye-level: Innovation in animal-assisted psychotherapy for children and adolescents”. This project is a cooperation between the Messerli Research Institute (MRI) in Vienna and the University Hospital of Psychiatry Zurich (PUK) that aims to study and further develop animal-assisted interventions in child and youth psychotherapy from an interdisciplinary perspective. The project, funded by the Messerli Foundation, critically explores the potential of animals as co-therapists, taking into account the complex interests of both animals and clients. We propose a cautious approach where animals are only part of psychotherapeutic interventions if they are motivated and capable of taking on an active role in building a “therapeutic” relationship. This interdisciplinary project integrates animal behaviour and cognition, animal welfare science, animal ethics and psychology and applies philosophical-conceptual, empirical and naturalistic-therapeutic methodologies. Its research is structured as three work packages: First, we review the literature on animal-assisted interventions in psychotherapy. Second, we develop and implement therapeutic interventions that open up relevant topics with the aid of animals, without, however, necessarily relying on the use of actual animals. Instead, animals will appear in various interactive multimedia formats tailored to (1) the needs of especially vulnerable children and adolescents and (2) the overarching aim of caring and just human-animal relations. Third, we will study and evaluate these new formats as well as traditional approaches to animal-assisted therapies that involve animals more directly. We hope to advance “therapeutic” human-animal relations at eye-level that are mutually beneficial and conducive to a culture of mutual care.

Your responsibilities

  • Conduct empirical research on animal-assisted and animal-ethics-themed interventions in psychotherapy with children and adolescents, with a particular focus on social interactions, therapeutic processes, animal welfare, and animal ethics
  • Investigate the contribution of animals or animal-ethics-themed interventions to therapeutic processes and the impact of these interventions on participating animals as well as the human-animal relationship
  • Design and oversee data collection and analysis, including observational, video-based, quantitative and qualitative approaches, as well as the exploration of automated and AI-assisted analysis methods, as appropriate to the research questions
  • Provide scientific and methodological guidance to the two Zurich PhD students and contribute to the supervision and training of undergraduate and graduate students
  • Publishing in peer-reviewed journals and presenting results at national and international scientific conferences
  • Contribute actively to interdisciplinary collaboration between Zurich and Vienna, including joint publications, project coordination, meetings, research visits, scientific events and reporting activities

Your profile

Required

  • Completed doctorate (PhD) in psychology, psychotherapy research, animal behaviour, animal welfare science or other fields related to the hosting project
  • Strong record of empirical research and peer-reviewed scientific publications relevant to the project
  • Solid methodological expertise in the study of social interaction and therapeutic processes, including quantitative research and statistical analysis
  • Experience in interdisciplinary research and collaboration, ideally in international research settings
  • Experience in supervising or mentoring students and junior researchers
  • Excellent English communication skills and very good German skills for research activities in the Zurich clinical setting
  • Ability to work independently, coordinate research activities and collaborate constructively in an interdisciplinary team

Nice to have

  • Experience with video-based behavioural coding, automated video analysis, machine learning or AI-assisted methods for analysing behavioural patterns and social interaction dynamics
  • Research or clinical experience in child and adolescent psychotherapy, developmental psychology or work with vulnerable patient populations
  • Experience in animal-assisted interventions, dog behaviour and animal training
  • Knowledge of animal welfare and animal ethics, including interdisciplinary work bridging the sciences and humanities
  • Experience with systematic or scoping reviews and mixed-methods research
  • Interest in physiological and neurobiological processes underlying social interactions and therapeutic change (e.g., cardiovascular or brain activity)
  • Swiss German comprehension
