Azienda e contesto

  • Gain insight into how financial services organizations operate and the economic and industry challenges they are facing
  • Drive effective and reliable financial and non-financial reporting as a forward-looking auditor with in-depth business process and internal controls understanding
  • Develop expertise in financial services regulations (e.g., AMLO-FINMA) and support organizations in meeting evolving regulatory requirements.
  • Conduct regulatory audits that identify compliance gaps, assess control effectiveness, and drive remediation strategies to ensure adherence to supervisory expectations and industry standards.
  • Develop people early on in your career and build a broad professional network
  • Bring your fresh ideas and pioneer thinking to the forefront early in your career at Deloitte
  • Go a clear, long-term career path and receive first-class training for your professional development, including support to qualify as Swiss certified public accountant (Swiss CPA). ( https://www.expertsuisse.ch/ausbildung ) What you bring to succeed
  • You hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Business Administration or Economics (ideally with a specialization in Accounting, Banking, Finance, or Controlling) and preferably have previous internship or work experience
  • You have excellent analytical competencies and are interested in understanding the world of banking and finance
  • You enjoy solving complex challenges in a team and are interested in the financial market, as well as innovation, and transformation (including but not limited to data analytics)
