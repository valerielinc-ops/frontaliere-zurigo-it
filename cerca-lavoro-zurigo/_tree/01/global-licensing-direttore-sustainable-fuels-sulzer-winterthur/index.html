Descrizione completa

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

We are looking for a Director Licensing Sustainable Fuels to join our Chemtech division.

In this role, you will lead the strategy and execution of our Sustainable Fuels product line, covering BioFlux (SAF/HVO), ethanol, biodiesel and electrification with VoltaSplit. You will shape the growth and competitiveness of a global licensing business at the heart of the energy transition.

What you will do

  • Define the Sustainable Fuels product line strategy and drive its execution and growth.
  • Shape and grow the global licensing pipeline together with Sales and Marketing.
  • Develop competitive offerings for SAF/HVO, ethanol and biodiesel, including electrification with VoltaSplit.
  • Drive competitiveness through benchmarking, cost optimization, pricing input and supply-chain development.
  • Steer process development, the R&D roadmap and intellectual property activities.
  • Develop strategic technology and commercial partnerships and evaluate emerging technologies.

What you need to succeed

  • Master’s degree in Chemical Engineering, Process Engineering or a related field.
  • More than 9 years of relevant professional experience.
  • Strong expertise in renewable fuels, refining or process technologies.
  • Proven experience in technology licensing, business development or product line management.
  • Strong commercial mindset combined with technical credibility and execution drive.
  • Ability to influence and collaborate effectively across functions and with external partners.
  • Fluent English and willingness to travel internationally approximately 25–50%.

What we offer

  • The opportunity to shape a global licensing business at the heart of the energy transition.
  • Work with proprietary and differentiated technologies, including BioFlux and VoltaSplit.
  • Broad influence across technology, partnerships and market development.
  • High visibility with global customers, partners and leadership.
  • Opportunities to grow your career within a well-established global company.

What we offer you

  • 30 days holiday + service days
  • Pension scheme & healthcare support
  • Parking or public transport allowance
  • Employee discounts on shopping, entertainment & lifestyle
  • Global career development opportunities
  • Innovative and agile work environment
  • Central Winterthur location, 5 minutes from the train station
