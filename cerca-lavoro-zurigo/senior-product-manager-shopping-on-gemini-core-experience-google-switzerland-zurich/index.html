Responsabilità principali

  • Define and drive the product roadmap for shopping capabilities within Gemini, identifying user needs and high-impact opportunities for conversational shopping.
  • Guide features from initial concept, UX exploration, and prototyping through launch and continuous iteration at Google scale.
  • Partner with UX and Engineering to design intuitive conversational paradigms, interactive product cards, visual browsing, and agentic workflows (e.g., automated comparison, personalized curation, streamlined checkout).
  • Establish evaluation frameworks, quality benchmarks, and safety/grounding standards to ensure shopping recommendations in Gemini are accurate, unbiased, trustworthy, and actionable.
  • Partner seamlessly across Google Commerce, Gemini and Merchant platforms to drive strategic alignment, technical integration, and shared Objectives and Key Results (OKRs).

Requisiti principali

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 8 years of experience in product management or a related technical role.
  • 3 years of experience taking technical products from conception to launch (e.g., ideation to execution, end-to-end, 0 to 1, etc).
  • Experience integrating generative AI tools or LLM interfaces into workflows.

Processo di candidatura

  • Experience building AI-native consumer products, conversational AI interfaces, or applications leveraging Generative AI, LLMs, and agentic workflows (e.g., tool use, grounding, structured outputs).
  • Experience in e-commerce, digital marketplaces, or consumer discovery/search products.
