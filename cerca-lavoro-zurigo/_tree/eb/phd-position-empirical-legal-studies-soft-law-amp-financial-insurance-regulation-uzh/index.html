Descrizione completa

University of Zurich - Law Faculty - Chair for Private Law, Comparative Law & Private International Law

Start of employment July 1, 2027 (or – optionally – February 1, 2027).

The Ph.D. position is part of a research project led by Prof. Heiss and Dr. Ostrowska. The project and the Ph.D. position are funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF). They are focused on examining how non-binding (soft law) supervisory instruments operate in practice in financial (insurance) regulation and why regulated insurance companies comply with supervisory soft law. The key issue is how soft law can be designed to be both effective and, in fact, non-binding.

Your responsibilities

  • Co-design and implement a comparative mixed-method empirical study of compliance with supervisory soft law in selected jurisdictions.
  • Develop an expert survey and design semi-structured expert interviews.
  • Analyze survey data using appropriate quantitative methods.
  • Analyze interview data using appropriate qualitative methods.
  • Contribute to the legal and comparative analysis of national supervisory instruments included in the empirical study.
  • Prepare ethics documentation, data-management plans and other research materials.
  • Contribute to academic publications and complete a project-based doctoral dissertation at the Law Faculty of the University of Zurich.

Your profile

  • Master's degree in law, economics, political science, sociology, public policy, empirical legal studies, or another relevant social-science discipline.
  • Excellent written and spoken English.
  • Ability to work in an international environment, reliable, independent and analytical working style.
  • Demonstrated training or experience in empirical social-science methods.
  • Training in or experience with at least one of the following: survey research, semi-structured interviews, quantitative data analysis, qualitative coding/analysis.
  • Ability and willingness to engage professionally with senior practitioners and regulated firms.
  • Strong interest in financial markets regulation (particularly insurance), regulatory compliance, enforcement, regulatory governance, behavioral responses to regulation, organizational decision-making, and empirical legal studies.
