Field Sales Engineer

Southeast (VA, FL, GA, NC) Full-Time | Exempt

Make an Impact at Tecan

Are you passionate about enabling scientific discovery through advanced laboratory automation and liquid handling technologies? Join our team as a Sales Engineer, Liquid Handling , where you will play a critical role in driving growth across the Northeast territory.

You will act as a trusted advisor, helping customers translate complex application needs into innovative automation solutions.
