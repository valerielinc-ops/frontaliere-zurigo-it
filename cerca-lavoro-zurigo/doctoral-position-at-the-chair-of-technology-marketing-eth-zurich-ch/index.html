Doctoral Position at the Chair of Technology Marketing

100%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken

We are recruiting Doctoral Position for a cross-border research project (funded by SNSF’s & DFG’s WEAVE scheme) on Virtual Reality, jointly hosted at ETH Zurich (Chair of Technology Marketing) and the TUM Campus Munich (Professorship of Marketing & Technology).

  • The SNF & DFG funded project investigates how people perceive time and space in immersive virtual environments, approaches related questions in an interdisciplinary manner (i.e., at the intersection of psychology, technology, and human–computer interaction).
  • We welcome candidates with a strong interest in fundamental research and, ideally, prior experience in VR research.
  • The project duration is set for three years. Job description

  • Primary emphasis on building and engineering immersive VR environments and experimental apparatus, while the candidate will be involved in developmental tasks, they should have a strong interest in fundamental research, and have a general entrepreneurial mindset
