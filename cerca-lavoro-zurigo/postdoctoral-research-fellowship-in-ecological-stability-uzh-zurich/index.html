Processo di candidatura

  • Owen Petchey at the University of Zurich invites applications for a Postdoctoral Research Fellowship of up to three years' duration, 100%.
  • The fellowship provides an opportunity for an early-career researcher to develop and pursue an original programme of research that advances understanding and prediction of ecological stability.
  • Research proposals should align with the group's broad scientific interests, including the development and application of concepts and tools such as response diversity.
  • The group investigates the determinants of ecological stability in temporal and or spatial dimensions, through a combination of ecological theory, mathematical modelling, experimental ecology, and the analysis of ecological observations and experiments.
  • We are interested in understanding how ecological systems respond to environmental change and in improving our ability to predict ecological dynamics across scales.
  • Applicants must have been awarded their PhD no more than four years before the application deadline.
  • We welcome applications from researchers who can demonstrate: a compelling vision for future research;
  • creativity and initiative in developing scientific ideas; a strong record of research achievement relative to career stage;
