  • 5 years of experience with software development in one or more general-purpose programming languages (e.g., Python, C++).
  • Experience building and maintaining distributed systems, cloud APIs, and mobile or embedded software development kits (SDKs).
  • Experience with AI/ML technologies, Large Language Models (LLMs) or Natural Language Understanding (NLU).
