Responsabilità principali

  • Develop and execute Shopping strategy in collaboration with other product leaders across the company, identifying key growth opportunities and driving innovation.
  • Maintain a deep understanding of the engaged landscape and emerging trends in retail, including the latest technologies and consumer behavior.
  • Collaborate with other teams across Google, including product, engineering, marketing, legal, and finance, to ensure alignment and integration with the overall business objectives.
  • Inspire engineering teams to innovate, invent, implement and deploy complex software solutions, especially in regards to modernizing the architecture through Machine Learning (ML) and Large Language Models (LLMs).
  • Cultivate leadership talent through effective mentoring, coaching, succession planning and strategies for key talent in a fast growth, highly engaged economic environment.

Requisiti principali

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 10 years of experience in product management or related technical role.
  • 5 years of experience taking technical products from conception to launch (e.g., ideation to execution, end-to-end, 0 to 1, etc).
  • 5 years of experience in people management, with technical leadership.

Azienda e contesto

  • As a Product Management leader for Consumer Shopping, you will drive the end-to-end product goal and strategy for key strategic priorities within Google’s consumer shopping organization.
  • In this role, you will serve as a foremost expert in the shopping and e-commerce industry as it leads an organization of product managers dedicated to creating seamless, innovative shopping journeys for users worldwide.
  • Users come first at Google.
  • Nowhere is this more important than on our Advertising and Commerce team: we believe that ads and commercial information can be highly useful to our users if that information is relevant to what our users wish to find or do.
  • Advertisers worldwide use Google Ads to promote their products
  • publishers use AdSense to serve relevant ads on their website
