Descrizione completa

Your role Interested in working in financial services, have analytical skills and know how to tell a story with data? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint?

We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Internship Program within our Group Internal Consulting team, focusing on projects in Personal & Corporate (P&C) and Global Wealth Management (GWM).

You’ll get to

  • support strategic transformation initiatives across UBS P&C and GWM by analyzing business challenges, identifying improvement opportunities, and helping deliver tangible outcomes for clients and stakeholders
  • apply structured problem-solving and analytical thinking to develop actionable recommendations and innovative solutions across business and integration projects
  • transform complex information and data into clear insights, compelling presentations, and executive-ready recommendations for senior stakeholders
  • collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive project execution, support stakeholder engagement, and contribute to the successful delivery of strategic priorities

Your team You'll join Group Internal Consulting (GIC) Client Coverage Switzerland, a team located in Zurich, at the heart of UBS's transformation agenda. We work with stakeholders across Personal & Corporate (P&C) and Global Wealth Management (GWM) to tackle strategic challenges, drive change, and turn ideas into tangible business outcomes. As part of the firm’s Change Management unit, in GIC you'll gain exposure to senior leaders, work on impactful projects, and collaborate with consultants across UBS's global internal consulting network.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor's degree, and graduated no longer than 12 months ago (bachelor's or master's degree)
  • has a competitive minimum GPA of 4.5 according to the Swiss grading system
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • enjoys thinking through problems in a structured way and contributing ideas and solutions
  • is comfortable using Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel and has an interest in analyzing information, structuring content, and communicating insights clearly
  • demonstrates initiative and a willingness to take ownership of tasks while seeking guidance when needed
  • demonstrates good organizational skills and a proactive approach to managing priorities
  • has excellent English language skills, German is a plus

Start date: 1 February 2027

Your program This paid internship will last six months. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You'll be given mentors along the way and will have the chance to get to know some of the most senior members of our teams. You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.
