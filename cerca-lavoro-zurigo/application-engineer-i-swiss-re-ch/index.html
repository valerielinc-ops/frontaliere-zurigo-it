About the role Are you looking for a challenge that encourages creative thinking and allows you to build on your strong software development and testing experience? Do you want to play a pivotal role in enabling the seamless coexistence of multiple agile implementation projects and a mission‑critical business application operating on a highly integrated, shared platform? Do you believe that while machines execute repetitive tasks, people solve problems—and truly exceptional people prevent those problems from occurring in the first place? If so, you are exactly the kind of talent we are looking for. We are seeking a technically curious, interface‑savvy engineer to join our team. The candidate will take ownership of engineering activities spanning development, system integration, and technical analysis within the Subledger domain—working across diverse technology stacks and contributing to the harmonization of interconnected systems. About the team Globally distributed team of engineers and BA's (Zurich, US, India, Slovakia) working with global stakeholders and significant stakeholder base across locations.

About you

Essential

  • The ideal candidate should have expertise in software engineering and SAP S4HANA.
  • 3+ years of hands-on experience in designing and implementing software solutions.
  • Proficiency in object oriented programming languages, database tables/structures and SQL.
  • Proven track record across all phases of application development: requirements gathering, architecture, implementation, maintenance, testing, and documentation
  • Bachelor's degree in computer science, Information Technology, or related field.
  • A mindset geared toward business analysis, not just development.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills and experience working in an agile environment
  • Able to deal with ambiguity and influence positive outcomes.

Pro-active, forward-looking mind set and exceptional delivery focus.

Nice to Have

  • Exposure to Bank Analyzer or FPSL
  • Experience working in hybrid environments involving both technical and functional stakeholders.
  • Experience in Microsoft Azure and Postgres
  • Experience in Java
