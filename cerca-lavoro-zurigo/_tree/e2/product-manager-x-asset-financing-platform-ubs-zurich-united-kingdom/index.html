Product Manager – X-Asset Financing Platform — UBS

Descrizione completa

Key responsibilities Do you enjoy turning complex financing and collateral workflows into strategic, data-driven platforms? Are you excited by the opportunity to shape a new cross-asset financing platform from the ground up, working directly with traders, structurers and quants?

We're looking for a Product Manager to

  • own the product vision, roadmap and end-to-end delivery of a strategic X-Asset Financing platform supporting Structured Financing and Fund Financing businesses
  • lead the transition from vendor tools, spreadsheets and end-user computing (EUC) solutions to an integrated, controlled in-house platform built on our strategic ACQA analytics platform
  • translate complex requirements from Front Office, Credit, Risk and Operations into clear user stories, acceptance criteria and a prioritised product backlog
  • shape capabilities for collateral and loan data ingestion, data standardisation, borrowing-base and covenant calculations, eligibility and concentration tests, trigger monitoring, and trade- and portfolio-level analytics and dashboards
  • make sure the platform is designed for data quality, reconciliation, auditability and historical snapshots, with controls built into the workflow
  • drive EUC remediation and decommissioning, working with control functions to agree which tools move to the strategic platform and when
  • coordinate a multi-location delivery team of engineers and quants across London, New York and Shanghai, track progress and remove blockers across business and technology
  • prioritise investment based on business value, risk reduction, control and regulatory requirements, and delivery dependencies
  • drive adoption and continuous improvement by working closely with desk users and validating solutions against real workflows

The team You'll be working in the IB Quantitative Analytics team, within Structured Products and Solutions, in [London / Zurich]. We're launching a significant, multi-year investment to build a strategic platform across structured and fund financing, bringing together collateral management, portfolio monitoring, stress testing and risk analytics in one place. As Product Manager, you'll lead the product side of the programme. You'll work with a dedicated group of engineers and quantitative analysts and partner every day with Structured Financing and Fund Financing desks, Credit, Risk, Operations and Technology.

Your skills and experience

  • proven experience as a Product Manager or Product Owner delivering front-office, risk or data platforms in investment banking or financial services
  • a strong understanding of financing businesses, ideally in one or more of: structured financing, asset-backed lending, borrowing-base facilities, fund financing, securitisation or collateral management
  • experience defining and delivering data-heavy platforms, including ingestion of non-standard data, data models, calculation and rules engines, validation and exception management
  • the ability to translate complex business and credit requirements into executable delivery plans and a well-governed backlog, using Agile methods
  • excellent stakeholder management, with confidence engaging senior Front Office, Credit, Risk and Technology stakeholders
  • an understanding of risk, control, audit and regulatory requirements, including EUC governance and data classification
  • experience leading delivery across distributed teams in multiple time zones
  • ideally, experience replacing vendor or spreadsheet-based solutions with strategic in-house capabilities
  • ideally, knowledge of portfolio surveillance, covenant monitoring, exposure and concentration analytics, or loan-level analytics
  • ideally, hands-on use of SQL, Python or AI-assisted tools to analyse data and prototype solutions

You are

  • a platform thinker who builds reusable capabilities rather than one-off solutions
  • able to simplify complex operational processes through automation and good product design
  • pragmatic, balancing speed of delivery with control and robustness
  • a clear communicator, comfortable working with traders, quants, engineers and senior stakeholders
  • collaborative, outcome-focused and comfortable in a fast-moving, evolving environment
