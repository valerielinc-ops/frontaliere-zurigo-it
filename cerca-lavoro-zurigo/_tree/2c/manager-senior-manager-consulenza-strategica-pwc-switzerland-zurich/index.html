Your Team Strategy& is the global strategy consultancy of PwC. Your Impact As a Manager in the Strategy & Transformation Team, you work with leading national and international organizations across various industries. Together with your team, you develop strategies and concepts along the entire value chain – from business and operating models to technology and digital strategies, and transformation programs. You lead (part) projects, manage interdisciplinary teams, and act as an interface between clients, partners, and internal stakeholders. In doing so, you work closely with colleagues from different consulting areas and utilize the expertise of our global network to develop and implement holistic solutions. Furthermore, you take on a mentoring role, promote the development of your colleagues, and actively contribute to an open, collaborative team culture. Through training, you continuously deepen your methods, leadership, and industry competence. In projects, you analyze complex issues of our clients, structure problems, and develop strategic solutions with sustainable added value. Typical topics include:

  • Development of corporate, business unit, and digital strategies
  • Redesign of business and operating models
  • Planning and implementation of transformation and change programs
  • Support for M&A initiatives, carve-outs, and portfolio strategies
  • Optimization of organizations, processes, and value chains
  • Use of data, analytics, and new technologies for decision support

You bear responsibility for the results of your projects, ensure quality as well as budget and deadline adherence, and present results at the C-Level. Furthermore, you actively support the acquisition of new projects, develop proposals, and further develop existing client relationships.

Your Skill Set

  • Academic Background: Above-average university degree (Master, MBA, or PhD) in Economics, (Business) Informatics, (Business) Engineering, Natural or Social Sciences, Statistics/Data Science, or a comparable field.
  • Professional Experience: Several years of experience in a top strategy consultancy or in responsible corporate functions, ideally with a focus on strategy, transformation, digitalization, or performance improvement. Experience in leading projects as well as in proposal development and project acquisition.
  • International Experience: International experience through study or work and confident movement in international and intercultural contexts.
  • Communication Skills: Very good oral and written communication skills in German and English. Ability to clearly structure complex content and present it persuasively to decision-making and management bodies.
  • Analytical Competence: Strongly developed analytical skills and structured working style with the ability to quickly grasp complex facts and translate them into actionable recommendations.
  • Digital Affinity: Proficient in using common tools (e.g., Excel, PowerPoint) and open to modern data analysis and AI tools; knowledge of data processing, statistical software, or programming languages is an advantage.
  • Personal Profile: High self-initiative, sense of responsibility, and team orientation. Strong customer orientation and a commitment to achieving sustainable impact for clients.
