Descrizione completa

About the Role The AI Governance Lead is responsible for establishing, operating and continuously improving the enterprise-wide AI Governance framework. The role ensures that AI solutions, including Generative and Agentic AI, are introduced and operated in a responsible, compliant and business-oriented manner. The AI Governance Lead serves as the central coordination point between Data & AI, Business, Legal & Compliance, Privacy, Cybersecurity, Enterprise Architecture, IT Platforms and other relevant functions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Establish, maintain and continuously develop the enterprise AI Governance framework, policies, standards and guardrails
  • Design and operate governance processes covering intake, risk classification, assessment, approval, deployment, monitoring and retirement of AI solutions
  • Establish and operate the central AI Register and governance of approved AI tools
  • Coordinate cross-functional AI assessments with Legal, Compliance, Privacy, Cybersecurity, Architecture, Platform and Procurement
  • Establish and coordinate AI governance bodies, including mandates, decision rights, escalation paths and regular governance reviews
  • Define governance requirements for Generative AI and Agentic AI, including human oversight, model quality, permissions, logging, monitoring and lifecycle controls
  • Integrate AI Governance into existing architecture, procurement, security, platform and operational processes
  • Coordinate periodic reviews, exceptions and escalations and maintain appropriate governance evidence
  • Provide management reporting on AI portfolio, risks, compliance, costs and governance effectiveness
  • Drive AI governance awareness, guidance and role-based training together with relevant functions.

This expands Sika's existing AI policy, inventory, risk-assessment process, Governance Committee and Expert Team into a more systematic enterprise operating model.

Your Skills and Experience

  • University degree in Business, Technology, Information Systems, Data/AI, Risk Management or a related field
  • Several years of experience in AI/Data Governance, Technology Governance, Risk Management or a comparable field
  • Good understanding of AI, Generative AI and emerging Agentic AI technologies and associated risks
  • Knowledge of relevant AI regulation and governance standards, ideally including EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF
  • Experience designing and implementing cross-functional governance processes in an international organization
  • Strong stakeholder management and ability to coordinate Business, Technology and Control Functions
  • Pragmatic, structured and hands-on mindset, with the ability to balance risk, compliance, business value and speed
  • Proficiency in English
