Descrizione completa

For our technology team in Zurich, we are looking for a Team Lead – Software Engineering & Atlassian Platforms to lead people, coordinate priorities and ensure reliable delivery. Combining a strong engineering background with hands-on Atlassian expertise, you will guide technical decisions, support complex challenges and drive internal process digitalisation using Java/Kotlin, Kafka, Spring Boot and OpenShift (Docker). You will own the direction, operation and evolution of Jira, Jira Service Management, Confluence and Assets, with a focus on the Data Center-to-Cloud transition and a sustainable operating model. By aligning stakeholders and technology, you will enable secure, scalable, user-focused solutions in a collaborative, inclusive and innovative environment. Ready to grow and take ownership? We’d love to hear from you.

What would your role be?

  • Lead and develop the team: lead, coach and develop Atlassian engineers and software specialists. Create an inclusive, collaborative and delivery-focused environment with clear objectives, ownership and development paths.
  • Coordinate priorities and delivery: translate strategy and demand into a transparent roadmap and manageable backlog. Coordinate resources, dependencies, operational work and change initiatives, and remove obstacles for the team.
  • Own stakeholder relationships: act as the primary contact and escalation point for business and technology stakeholders. Communicate decisions, risks, progress, service quality and priorities clearly, including at senior-management level.
  • Shape the Atlassian platform: own and evolve the strategy, architecture and operating model for Jira, Jira Service Management and Assets. Ensure stable, secure and compliant services, effective lifecycle management and measurable service quality.
  • Drive the Cloud transformation: guide the technical and organisational transition from Atlassian Data Center to Cloud, including application assessments, marketplace app rationalisation, migration decisions, governance and organisational readiness.
  • Provide engineering leadership: guide solution design, integrations, automation and platform standardization. Establish pragmatic development practices, code quality, testing, documentation and review standards.
  • Stay selectively hands-on: contribute to complex configuration, development, integration and troubleshooting topics when your involvement adds value, without becoming the delivery bottleneck.

What are we looking for?

  • Leadership and coordination: Proven experience leading technical teams or senior specialists. You set direction, make decisions, delegate effectively, resolve conflicts and help people grow.
  • Software engineering foundation: Several years of practical experience designing, building, testing and maintaining enterprise applications. Strong understanding of clean code, APIs, integrations, automated testing, CI/CD and maintainable architecture.
  • Relevant development skills: Hands-on experience with Java and/or Kotlin and modern development frameworks such as Spring Boot. Experience modernizing codebases and reviewing implementation approaches is valuable.
  • Atlassian expertise: Practical knowledge of Jira, Jira Service Management, Confluence and Assets in a complex enterprise environment. You understand configuration, workflows, permissions, automation, integrations and operational platform needs.
  • Platform and service mindset: Experience with platform governance, service management, operational processes, security and compliance. You connect technical choices with customer value, reliability, cost and risk.
  • Communication: Clear, pragmatic and trustworthy communication across engineering, operations, business and management. Fluent English is required
  • German is highly desirable.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels.
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit.
  • A role with real influence over the team, platform and transformation roadmap.
  • A collaborative environment with direct communication and short decision paths.
  • Learning, development and mentoring opportunities.
  • Modern technology and close collaboration across business and technology.

We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes. Even if you do not have absolutely everything we have asked for, if this role sounds like a good fit for you, we encourage you to apply.
