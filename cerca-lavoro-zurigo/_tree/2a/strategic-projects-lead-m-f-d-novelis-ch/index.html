Position Overview

Who we are

Novelis is driven by its purpose of Shaping a Sustainable World Together. As a global leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest recycler of aluminum, we partner with customers in the automotive, beverage can, aerospace and specialties industries to deliver solutions that improve the benefits of balanced lightweight aluminum. We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling plants as well as foundries across 29 facilities in 9 countries throughout North America, South America, Europe, and Asia with 12,750 employees worldwide. The company is headquartered in Atlanta, USA.

Our customers include some of the largest and best-known brands in the world, including Coca-Cola, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry with its sustainability and recycling efforts. For this role, you can be based at our European Headquarters in Küsnacht , Switzerland; at one of our German locations in Göttingen, Koblenz, Nachterstedt, Ohle, Stuttgar t or Voerde ; or at one of our Italian locations in Pieve or Bresso .

As Strategic Projects Lead, you will play a key role in shaping and advancing Novelis Europe’s strategic agenda. You will lead high-impact strategic projects from definition through recommendation and implementation, working across functions and closely with the Novelis Europe Management Team. The role combines strategic thinking with hands-on problem solving across topics such as growth, profitability, productivity and sustainability, translating market and business insights into clear priorities and actions.

Responsibilities & Qualifications

What awaits you

  • Lead strategic projects end-to-end , defining the strategic question and approach and guiding cross-functional teams from analysis through recommendation and implementation
  • Shape the strategic plan for Novelis Europe and lead targeted strategy reviews and deep dives for selected business segments
  • Help define and drive key regional priorities to deliver Novelis Europe’s long-term strategic objectives
  • Assess new growth opportunities and markets through market, customer and competitive analysis, translating insights into regional growth and investment recommendations
  • Partner closely with senior leaders and cross-functional stakeholders , including the Novelis Europe Management Team, to challenge thinking, build alignment and turn strategic recommendations into action

How you convince us

  • You hold a Bachelor’s degree or higher; a Master’s degree or MBA is a plus
  • You bring at least 3 years of relevant professional experience , ideally in a top-tier management consultancy; experience in the industrial, manufacturing or automotive sectors is a plus
  • You have a proven track record of leading cross-functional projects , engaging senior stakeholders and delivering in complex, fast-paced environments
  • You combine strategic thinking, intellectual curiosity and strong analytical skills , translating business performance, customer insights and market trends into actionable recommendations
  • You can structure complex problems and communicate insights clearly and convincingly , supporting fact-based management decisions
  • You bring a humble, collaborative and people-oriented mindset , building trusted relationships across all levels of the organization
  • You are fluent in English
  • German or Italian is a plus
  • You are willing to travel up to 40% of the time
